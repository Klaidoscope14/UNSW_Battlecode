2. HOW VIBING++ PLAYED THIS MAP
 - Opening tempo: 16 splits and 36 pearls in rounds 1-30 (us 11 / 12). Reached 40 dragons at r47
   and their plateau of 60-62 dragons at r71. We only reached 20 at r38 and 40 at r97.
 - Fleet plateau: held ~61 dragons from r75 to r350. That is 62 = their cap on the 64-unit limit.
 - Speed: 14% of their move orders were multi-step (2-3 cells using free steps); ours 1.1%.
   Their dragons were longer: 24% of non-queen dragon-turns at length >= 5 (ours 6%).
 - Raiding: they ate 560 pearls on OUR half of the map; we ate 15 on theirs.
 - Spawn camping: at the moment a pearl appeared, their nearest head was a median 3 cells away
   (ours 12). 19% of all spawns had a Vibing++ head within 1 cell already.
 - Self-destruct recycling: 313 deliberate deaths (they order an illegal "split off 1"). 84% of
   them happened while the fleet sat at its 60-62 plateau. The 725 pearls dropped were eaten 92%
   by their own dragons, usually within 2-7 rounds.
 - Half-splits: from r100 on, dragons of 20-40 split in two (59 splits with a child >= 10).
   Their final giant (dragon 669, length 62) was born at r348 as a 42-cell half-split.

3. QUEENS
 - Their queen: a split-factory early (length 4-5, splits off 2s), then grew 7 -> 22 between r100
   and r150 and reached 27 by r200. She roamed the whole map (351 distinct cells, up to 56 cells from
   her spawn; at r200 she sat at (14,59) inside OUR area) yet was inside our sprint reach in only
   2 of 396 rounds. We never seriously threatened her.
 - Our queen: split down to length 2 by r10 and never grew (4 pearls all game). She moved very
   little (54 distinct cells). Died r98: Vibing++ dragon 78 (length 2) shadowed her at 1-3 cells
   for 8 rounds. Our queen was judged "doomed" (no safe move) and took the "last-attack" option
   into it - a queen-for-a-length-2 trade that lost the game on the spot.

4. COMBAT
 - 75 head-on trades, almost exactly even (our length lost 231, theirs 255). 32 of their trades used
   a multi-step strike. Trades did not decide the game; economy and the queen kill did.

5. WHAT WENT WRONG FOR US
 - Self-inflicted deaths: 113 into kelp (dragons that judged themselves "doomed" walk into a wall),
   57 into bodies, 44 into themselves. Vibing++ had 4 non-combat deaths (plus their deliberate ones).
 - 14.9% of our moves were idle 2x2 circling (theirs 4.6%).
 - Queen at length 2 with no growth plan and no safe pocket.

6. TAKEAWAY FOR LARGE OPEN MAPS
 Big open maps are where their multi-step speed and pearl raiding matter most. Contest our own
 half (we lost 40% of our own-half pearls to them), use free steps, and keep the queen in our
 area out of reach of every enemy head's sprint range.
