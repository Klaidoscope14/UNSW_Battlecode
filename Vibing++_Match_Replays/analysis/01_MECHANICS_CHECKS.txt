MECHANICS CHECKS  -  engine-verified with unswbc 1.2.9 (latest on PyPI)
=========================================================================
Method: a scripted test bot (fixed command per dragon per round) on purpose-built test maps.
Replays were decoded with tools/replay_to_txt.mjs. No changes to the fleet bot.
Test bot and maps: session scratchpad, mech/ (mb/main.cpp, maps/t1-t4.map).

-------------------------------------------------------------------------
CHECK 1. SELF-DESTRUCT AND WHERE THE PEARLS LAND
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
Result: ANY invalid action kills the dragon at once ("no valid action"). Tested and all fatal:
  - "SPLIT 1" at lengths 2, 3, 4, 5, 8, 12, 13, 16
  - "SPLIT 2" from length 3 (parent would be left at 1)
  - "SPLIT 0"
  - printing nothing at all
  - "SUICIDE" (there is no such command)
  - a bare "MOVE" with no direction
  - a sprint longer than the dragon can pay for (see check 2)
  The queen can self-destruct the same way (tested on a length-5 queen).

Drop pattern: pearls appear on the head and on every second segment behind it (segment
indices 0, 2, 4, ...), so ceil(L/2) pearls. Examples from the test (head at the right):
  L2 -> head only                       L3 -> head + tail
  L5 -> head, seg 2, seg 4 (3 pearls)   L8 -> 4 pearls, two cells apart along the body
  L16 -> 8 pearls

Consequences:
 - Feeding geometry: a feeder lying straight across the queen's path leaves pearls on
   alternating cells. The queen collects ceil(L/2) pearls in L-1 steps by walking along where
   the body was.
 - Efficiency by feeder length: L3 -> 2 pearls (67%), L5 -> 3 (60%), L2 or L4 -> 50%.
   Odd lengths, especially 3, are the best feeders.
 - SAFETY for our own bot: an empty turn, a malformed command or an overlong sprint is instant
   death. Every code path must print exactly one valid action. Our existing fallback move must
   never be skipped (a crash, a timeout, an exception after printing nothing).

-------------------------------------------------------------------------
CHECK 2. STRIKES, SPRINT TRUNCATION AND THE EXACT STRIKE RANGE
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
Result:
 a) Kill after the target moved: our test queen (id 0) moved E to (6,5). A later-moving enemy of
    length 6, 4 cells away, ordered WWWW (2 free + 2 paid steps). Both died head-on.
    CONFIRMED: an enemy that moves later can strike our queen's NEW head cell.
 b) Moving into a head that has NOT moved yet this round is also a head-on (both die).
    So an earlier-moving dragon, including our queen, can also strike.
 c) Truncation: a length-8 dragon ordered 6 steps and collided on step 3. It died there,
    keeping its full length (4 pearls dropped); the rest of the order was dropped.
 d) Exact maximum steps per turn = ceil(L/4) free + (L-2) paid; the dragon ends at length 2.
    One step more and the WHOLE order is invalid and the dragon dies.
      L2: 1 step   (2 steps -> dies)
      L3: 2 steps  (3 -> dies)
      L4: 3 steps  (4 -> dies)
      L5: 5 steps legal, ends at L2   (6 -> dies)
      L6: 6 steps legal, ends at L2
      L9: 10 steps legal              (11 -> dies)
      L12: 13 steps legal             (14 -> dies)
 => Strike range R(L) = ceil(L/4) + L - 2 (path steps). This is the exact zone our queen must
    avoid ending her move in, for every enemy head that moves after her (all of them).

-------------------------------------------------------------------------
CHECK 3. QUEEN 2x2 BUNKER LOOP
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
Result:
 - A length-3 queen circling a 2x2 box survived all 500 rounds (4 cells, 1 always free).
 - A length-2 dragon circling a 2x2 also works indefinitely.
 - A length-4 queen filling the whole 2x2 died on her FIRST move ("hit itself"). Her tail still
   occupies the cell at the moment of the collision check.
 => A bunkered queen must be length 2 or 3. If she eats while bunkered (reaching 4) she must
    leave the loop or split 2 off first.
 => Not yet tested: whether guards can seal the cells around the loop so no enemy can reach a
    cell next to her head. Guards must move every turn too, so they would loop as well. This
    needs its own test when we design the bunker.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------
CHECK 4. LOCAL MAPS vs LADDER MAPS
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
The ladder .replay files (which embed the map) are no longer on disk, and the toolkit has no
map download. So every Vibing++ ladder log was checked against our local maps: starting bodies,
every move against local kelp, every portal jump against local portals, and every pearl against
local spawn tiles. Local control games on the same maps produce zero unexplained pearls.

 MATCH on every check (13 maps): Around UNSW, Australia, Autarky, Default, Devil, Islands, Maze,
   Portals, Stripes, Tower Defense, Trauma, Trophy, WeakHold.

 DIFFERENT on the ladder (4 maps):
  - Prisoners Dilemma: 10 starting dragons per side (local 6); 4 portal jumps where the local map
    has no portal.
  - SchoolTime: different kelp (ladder dragons crossed 8 edges that are kelp locally); 117 spawn
    cells that do not exist locally; 244 of the 326 local spawn tiles never spawned.
  - Slithery Fight: different pearl layout; 149 extra spawn cells; 153 of 331 local tiles never
    spawned (kelp and portals matched).
  - Queen of Spades: minor; 2 extra pearl cells at round 1, everything else consistent.

 Consequences:
  - Toolkit 1.2.9 is the latest, so "unswbc update" will not fix this; the ladder runs newer
    versions of at least these maps.
  - Our local benchmark results on SchoolTime, Slithery Fight and Prisoners Dilemma do not
    represent the ladder. Weight the other 13 maps more when judging changes.
  - It supports the plan to stay map-agnostic: map details can change without notice.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT THIS CHANGES IN THE IMPROVEMENT PLAN
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
 - Queen safety (#5) can use the exact formula R(L) = ceil(L/4)+L-2 as a hard rule.
 - Hunters (#28): a length-5 hunter already strikes 5 cells, length 9 strikes 10. Never order a
   sprint longer than R(L) - it kills our own dragon.
 - Feeding (#24): use length-3 feeders lying across the queen's path, so she eats both pearls
   2 cells apart. Queen self-destruct is possible but obviously never wanted.
 - Bunker (#9): the queen must be length 2-3 while looping; guard sealing still needs a test.
 - Recycling (#20): an illegal "SPLIT 1" is the self-destruct command.
