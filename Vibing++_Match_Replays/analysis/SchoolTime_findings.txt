4. TAKEAWAY
 On bunker maps the queen tie is guaranteed (both length 3), so the result is decided purely by
 longest dragon and total length. They have a deliberate plan for that (giant plus feeding); we do
 not. The 2x2 queen loop is also proof that a length-3 queen can circle a 2x2 safely all game,
 which supports the queen-bunker idea.
