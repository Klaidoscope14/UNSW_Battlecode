2. HOW VIBING++ PLAYED THIS MAP
 - This is the one map where we out-expanded them early: 31 vs 19 dragons at r50 and 42 vs 23
   pearls in r1-30. They overtook by r100 and capped at 60-62.
 - Their queen grew early (6 by r10, 9 by r75, 11 by r150) while splitting 2 off at length 8
   (r21, 32, 35, 40, 60). She was a growing factory, not a length-2 one.
 - 12% multi-step orders; 405 portal jumps by them vs our 25.
 - Endgame giant: their longest went 21 -> 47 -> 78 -> 100 over r250-500. The final giant
   (dragon 708) was born at r390 as a 43-cell split from the previous giant 579. That is a reversal:
   the old tail becomes the new head. It then cruised at 3 free steps a turn eating 57 pearls,
   47 of them self-destruct drops from their own dragons.
 - 323 self-destructs; 575 drops, 89% eaten by them.

3. QUEENS
 - Our queen (length 2) died r152 at (29,14). Vibing++ dragon 206 (length 3) was 2 cells away and
   used a 2-step strike (WW).
 - Their queen (length 11) died r233 at (47,14). Our dragon 394 (length 7, "cmb war regicide")
   sprinted 5 steps (SWSWS) with paid steps and traded itself for her. Our regicide works when a
   long-enough dragon is in range.

4. COMBAT
 - 133 trades, even (our length lost 417, theirs 388). 57 of their trades used multi-step strikes.
 - Our self-inflicted deaths: 78 walls ("doomed"), 26 self, 18 bodies.

5. WHAT WENT WRONG FOR US
 - The early lead did not compound. From r100 our fleet shrank (38 -> 4) while theirs held at 60.
 - After both queens died we had no plan to grow a long dragon; theirs went 21 -> 100 by feeding.

6. TAKEAWAY
 When both queens are dead the game is "longest dragon". They have a deliberate pipeline for it:
 grow one giant, feed it with self-destruct drops, and reverse it by splitting when it is boxed in.
