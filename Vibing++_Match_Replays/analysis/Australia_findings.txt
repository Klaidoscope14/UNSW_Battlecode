2. HOW VIBING++ PLAYED THIS MAP
 - Our economy never started: our fleet never passed 15 dragons and made only 40 splits all game.
   At pearl-spawn time our nearest head was a median 16 cells away; theirs 3.
 - They ate 1073 pearls on OUR half (we ate 130 there) and 906 on theirs (we ate 8).
 - Fastest movers of all 17 games: 24.5% of their move orders were multi-step. 28% of their
   non-queen dragon-turns were at length 9+, which gives 3 free steps a turn.
 - 321 self-destructs. 150 half-splits with a child >= 10, mostly after r300 (45 in r300-399,
   84 in r400-500). Late game is a split/recombine churn around a few big dragons.

3. QUEENS
 - Their queen stayed at length 4 for 200 rounds. She roamed far (up to 43 cells from spawn) but
   was inside our sprint reach in only 2 of 499 rounds. From r300 she grew 7 -> 49. 40 of her
   47 pearls were drops from their own self-destructing dragons (feeding).
 - Our queen (length 2-3) died r142 at (48,20). Vibing++ dragon 165 (length 5) was 4 cells away
   after our queen's move. It ordered a 4-step sprint (NNNE), paying segments, and hit her head-on.
   That is the core kill pattern: free + paid steps reach ceil(L/4) + (L-2) cells, here 5.

4. COMBAT
 - 37 trades; they were the longer side in 16, we in 11. Our length lost 121, theirs 139.
   25 of their trades used multi-step strikes.

5. WHAT WENT WRONG FOR US
 - The fleet stalled at ~10 dragons with ~0.03 pearls per dragon-turn (theirs ~0.09). Most of our
   turns were "col keep pearl" / "col nrm pearl": chasing pearls they reached first.
 - Our queen walked into the open where a length-5 hunter could sprint 4 cells to her.

6. TAKEAWAY
 On huge open maps their advantage is distance: spawn camping plus multi-step movement means they
 arrive first everywhere. If we cannot hold spawns we lose every tiebreak. The queen-feeding
 pipeline (self-destruct next to the queen) is how they reach queen length 49 without the queen
 farming herself.
