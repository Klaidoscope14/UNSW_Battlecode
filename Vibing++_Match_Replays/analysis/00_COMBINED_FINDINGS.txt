VIBING++ REVERSE-ENGINEERING REPORT  -  17 unrated games vs Low Cortisol (fleet v2.5.0)
=========================================================================================
Per-map details are in <Map>_findings.txt in this folder. Observations only; no code was changed.

HOW THIS WAS DONE
 - Every game was re-simulated from the logs. Starting bodies come from the official map files,
   then every move, eat, paid step, split and death in the log is replayed. Our own logged lengths
   ("L<n>" in our indicators) matched the rebuild on every turn of every game, so the positions
   and lengths used below are exact.
 - Team of each dragon: the starting ids alternate by team, and a split child inherits its
   parent's team. Note: "even = one team, odd = the other" is only true for starting dragons.
   Split children get the next global id, so about half of them have the other parity.
 - Caveats:
   . The Around_UNSW log stops at r397 (looks like a truncated copy).
   . The ladder Prisoners Dilemma has 10 starting dragons per side; our local dilemma.map has 6.
     The ladder may run newer map versions than our local copies.
   . Some file labels are approximate. For example, the Trauma and Portals games were lost on
     queen length at r500, and SchoolTime was a queen tie decided by longest dragon.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PART 1. THE BIG PICTURE IN NUMBERS (all 17 games)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 - Our queen died in 16 of 17 games (SchoolTime was the exception, where both queens bunkered).
   15 of the 16 deaths were head-on. She was length 2-3 in 13 of them.
 - Their queen died in 8 games:
   . 5 to our regicide (Islands, Maze, Queen of Spades, Trophy, Stripes)
   . 3 to her own self-destruct when boxed in (Devil, Prisoners Dilemma, WeakHold)
 - In 6 games their queen died BEFORE ours: Devil, Dilemma, WeakHold, Queen of Spades, Stripes,
   Maze. Keeping our queen alive (and one dragon alive) would have won all 6 at r500. We lost
   our queen 2-88 rounds later in every one.
 - Pearls eaten across all games: us 6,273 vs Vibing++ 16,981 (2.7x).
 - Head-on trades are even: 511 trades, our length lost 1,555 vs theirs 1,582. Fighting is not
   where we lose; economy and the queen are.
 - Non-combat deaths (not head-on):
   . us: 853 into walls/kelp, 480 into ourselves, 384 into bodies
   . them: 0 walls, 0 self, 92 bodies
   . them, deliberate: 4,023 self-destructs, 3 of them queens ("gave no valid action", Part 2.6)

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PART 2. THE VIBING++ PLAYBOOK (reverse-engineered)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.1 OPENING "FACTORY" (rounds 1-100)
 - Every dragon, the queen included, splits off 2 the moment it reaches length 4, sometimes 3 at
   length 5. Their queen did this 5-7 times in the first 100 rounds on Devil, Default, Trophy and
   WeakHold, staying at length 2-5.
 - Rounds 1-30 pearls eaten, them vs us:
   Devil 30/4, Dilemma 28/6, Default 14/1, Stripes 19/4, Portals 16/1, UNSW 36/12, Trophy 10/7.
   The count gap opens here and compounds.
 - "Production line" (Portals log r5-r29): a farmer walks a pearl lane, splits its tail off on
   the spot every time it reaches 4-5, and the child keeps farming the same lane. One lane fed a
   new dragon about every 3 rounds.

2.2 SPAWN CAMPING (pre-positioning by spawn countdown)
 - When a pearl appears, their nearest head is a median 2-3 cells away on every map. Ours is
   8-21 cells away.
 - 19-37% of all spawns already have one of their heads within 1 cell (ours 1-16%).
 - Their idle 2x2 circling happens next to spawn tiles: 85-95% of their loops are within 2 cells
   of a spawn tile on Tower Defense, Trauma, Devil, Trophy, Queen of Spades and Portals.
   Our idle loops mostly are NOT near spawns (SchoolTime: 31 of 534).
 - Tower Defense: a head was parked at the mouth of the centre field at r97, before the big r100
   spawn. Their fleet went 5 -> 26 in the next 50 rounds.

2.3 SPEED: FREE STEPS AND MEDIUM-LENGTH DRAGONS
 - Multi-step move orders as a share of all orders:
   . them: Australia 24.5%, UNSW 14%, SchoolTime 12.4%, Islands 11.9%, Slithery 9.3%
   . us: 0-2.8% on every map
 - On big maps they keep many dragons at length 5-12 (2-3 free steps a turn): 20-43% of their
   dragon-turns on UNSW, Australia and SchoolTime, against 6-11% of ours. Their giants cruise at
   3 free steps a turn.

2.4 RAIDING AND TERRITORY
 - Pearls eaten on OUR half, them vs us: Australia 1073 vs 130, UNSW 560 vs 838, Trauma 315 vs 63,
   Tower 246 vs 28, SchoolTime 533 vs 33. We almost never eat on their half (0-25 pearls), except
   Portals, Islands and Slithery.
 - Their share of the map (cells nearer their heads) typically goes from ~55% at r25 to 75-95%
   by r100-150. Portals was the only map where we took the map back.

2.5 FLEET SIZE
 - Big maps: a plateau of 60-62 dragons from r75-150 to about r350 (the unit limit is 64).
 - Small and medium maps: a plateau of one dragon per about 12-20 cells (Tower 42, Devil 31,
   WeakHold 36, Trophy 48, Trauma 59).
 - Our fleet peaked at 57 (Slithery) and was stuck at 2-15 dragons on 10 of the 17 maps.

2.6 DELIBERATE SELF-DESTRUCT ("split off 1" = illegal action = the dragon dies)
 - 4,023 deaths, every single one of them Vibing++. They always ask "split off 1", which is
   illegal at any length, so it is a deliberate kill switch.
 - A dead dragon drops ceil(L/2) pearls, so they convert length into pearls exactly where they
   want them. 89-100% of those drops are eaten by their own dragons, a median 3-5 rounds later.
 - Typical context: length 2-3; a teammate's head 1-3 cells away; enemies 10-17 cells away;
   only 1 free neighbour (pocket or corridor); and the dragon ate 2 rounds ago. It is used for
   three things:
   a) Trap recycling. A dragon in a dead end self-destructs instead of walking out or into a
      wall, and the teammate behind eats the drop. This is why they have 0 wall deaths and
      happily harvest maze dead ends (664 self-destructs on Maze).
   b) Fleet recycling at the cap. 60-84% of self-destructs on UNSW, Islands, Maze and Slithery
      happen while the fleet sits at its 60-62 plateau.
   c) Feeding the queen or the giant. The final longest Vibing++ dragon got most of its pearls
      from these drops:
        queen: Portals 57 of 61, Slithery 58 of 71, Tower 47 of 63, Australia 40 of 47,
               Trauma 27 of 41
        giant: SchoolTime 61 of 70, Islands 47 of 57, UNSW 19 of 20
   Efficiency of the conversion: length 2 -> 1 pearl (50%), 3 -> 2 (67%), 5 -> 3 (60%).
   They mostly use length 2-3.

2.7 QUEEN LIFECYCLE
 - r1 to about r150: split factory at length 2-5, roaming freely inside their own territory.
 - Growth window, varying by map (r100-150 UNSW, r150-200 Maze and Trauma, r150-250 Portals,
   r300+ Australia and Tower, r350+ Slithery): she grows fast, mainly by being FED drops.
   Peak queens: 64 Portals, 55 Tower, 52 Slithery, 49 Australia, 41 Trauma, 34 UNSW.
 - Safety: their queen was inside our sprint reach in 0-1% of rounds on 13 of 16 maps
   (Queen of Spades 5%, Stripes 12%). Own heads within 3 cells average 0.3-1.0, higher on small
   maps (Devil 1.55, Tower 0.97, Autarky 0.81). She is safe by distance, not by an escort.
 - Backup bodies: the queen splits a big child when long (Maze r281: 33 -> 7 plus a 26 child;
   Slithery r349: 29 -> 11 plus an 18 child).
 - Weakness: a boxed-in queen self-destructs like any other dragon (Devil r44, Dilemma r54,
   WeakHold r104).

2.8 QUEEN HUNTING (how they kill our queen)
 - Targeting: when our queen is within their 7x7 view, their moves go toward her 62% of the time.
   Toward our other dragons they move closer only 42% of the time (they avoid them).
   Queen of Spades: 75 closer vs 9 farther.
 - Stalkers: single small dragons shadow our queen at 1-3 cells for long periods (Trauma
   dragon 115 for 81 rounds, Queen of Spades dragon 8 for 33, Autarky 83 for 15, SchoolTime 17
   for 17). On small maps a loose net of 3-7 heads sits within 6 cells (Dilemma, Tower, Stripes).
 - The kill: the KAMIKAZE SPRINT. Queens move first in every round, so after our queen moves,
   any enemy dragon that can reach her new head cell this turn strikes. It uses free steps plus
   paid steps, and paying segments costs nothing because a head-on kills both anyway.
     Reach of a length-L dragon = ceil(L/4) + (L-2) cells:
       L2: 1, L3: 2, L4: 3, L5: 5, L6: 6, L7: 7, L8: 8, L9: 10.
   Logged killing orders, with the killer's distance after our queen's move:
     Australia NNNE (L5, 4 away)    Trauma EESSS (L6, 5 away)    Maze NENW (L9, 2 away)
     Slithery NNEE (L8, 4 away)     Devil NWW (L3, 3)            Islands WW (L3, 2)
     Default EE (L3, 2)             Dilemma NE (L3, 2)           Stripes ES (L3, 2)
     Trophy NE (L4, 2)
   Plus adjacent single steps (Autarky, Queen of Spades, Tower). Two more (UNSW, WeakHold) were
   our boxed-in queen taking "last-attack" into a length-2 stalker.
 - Over all head-on trades, 48% of their strikes were multi-step.

2.9 MID-GAME HALF-SPLITS AND ENDGAME GIANTS
 - From about r150, dragons of 20-40 split roughly in half. Children of 10+ cells: UNSW 59,
   Australia 150, Islands 41, Maze 34, SchoolTime 37, Slithery 56. This keeps every dragon fast
   and below the unit limit.
 - Endgame (r350+): fleet count falls (62 -> 30-45) while one giant (or the queen) absorbs drops:
   Islands 100, SchoolTime 89, Maze 73, UNSW 62. A giant that gets boxed in REVERSES by splitting
   off almost all of itself, since the child's head is the old tail (Islands r390: 43 off;
   Maze r476: 70 off; UNSW r348: 42 off).
 - The point: after a queen tie (SchoolTime) or both queens dead (Islands, Maze) the game goes to
   longest dragon, and they always have a giant ready. We never did (longest 3-22).

2.10 PORTALS
 - Portal jumps, them vs us: Islands 405/25, Maze 498/13, SchoolTime 413/0, Default 208/0,
   Trauma 202/3, Australia 174/0, Autarky 166/4. They use portals for expansion everywhere; we
   almost never do, except on Portals itself (366 vs 75), where we then collided with ourselves.

PART 3. OUR WEAKNESSES (seen in our own logged indicators)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 W1. Queen ends her move inside an enemy's sprint reach. The kill is almost always 2-5 cells, not
     adjacency. In 15 of 16 death rounds our queen was in "war" mode, and in 13 of them she was
     still heading for a pearl ("QUEEN cmb/prt war pearl").
 W2. Queen never grows. She sat at length 2-3 most of the game on 14 maps, with 0-23 pearls
     eaten. She has no feeding pipeline and cannot farm safely.
 W3. Queen dies AFTER we have already won the queen race (6 games, Part 1).
 W4. Economy collapse on maze, kelp and portal maps: 2-15 dragons all game on Australia,
     Autarky, Default, Devil, Dilemma, Queen of Spades, SchoolTime, Stripes, Tower, Trauma, Trophy
     and WeakHold. At spawn time our nearest head is 8-21 cells away; there is no spawn camping.
 W5. Idle circling: 10-34% of our moves are 2x2 loops away from spawns (SchoolTime 34%,
     WeakHold 29%, Portals 16.5%, UNSW 14.9%).
 W6. "Doomed" dragons walk into kelp: 853 wall deaths, 780 "doomed" turns on Slithery alone,
     sometimes at r4 for freshly split children. The length is dropped where the enemy may eat it.
 W7. Portal own-goals (Portals map): 30 pairs of our own dragons head-on through linked portals,
     and 174 self-collisions inside walled 2x2 pearl boxes entered through portals.
 W8. Hunters are too short. Our non-queen dragons are 70-97% length 2-4, so their sprint reach is
     1-3. Their queen sits 4+ cells from our heads and is safe. All 5 of our successful regicides
     came from length 3-7 dragons using 2-5-step orders.
 W9. Almost no multi-step movement (0-2.8% of orders). We give away 1-2 free cells a turn on
     every dragon of length 5+.
 W10. No endgame plan for "longest dragon" or "total length" when the queens are tied or dead.

PART 4. WHAT TO INTEGRATE / CHANGE / REMOVE IN OUR SUBMISSION (ranked by expected impact)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
(All map-agnostic: nothing below depends on a specific map.)

INTEGRATE
 I1. [Highest] Hard sprint-reach queen safety. The queen moves first, so every enemy head of
     length L (seen or recently reported) threatens all cells within ceil(L/4)+(L-2) PATH steps
     this turn. Treat that zone as forbidden for the queen's end-of-turn cell, not as a soft
     penalty. If no cell is outside every zone, prefer cells whose paths are blocked by our own
     bodies, then the fewest threats. This alone addresses 11 of the 16 queen deaths.
     (Our current paid-reach term is a soft 200k penalty capped at 16 cells; that is not enough.)
 I2. Queen bunker after the enemy queen dies (and in general when threatened). SchoolTime proves a
     length-3 queen circling a closed 2x2 is unkillable for 500 rounds when no enemy can reach the
     loop. Once their queen is confirmed dead, our only job is: queen alive plus at least one
     dragon alive. That would have converted 6 of these 17 losses into wins.
 I3. Queen feeding pipeline (their single biggest edge in queen length). From the growth window
     (they use about r150-300; we could tie it to "queen safe and fleet near its cap"), farmers
     travel to the queen's safe pocket and self-destruct 1 cell ahead of her head. A length-3
     farmer gives 2 pearls (67%). The queen grows without leaving safety. The same mechanism builds
     an endgame giant when the queens are tied or dead.
 I4. Trap recycling instead of wall deaths. A dragon that is doomed, or stuck in a dead end with
     nothing left to eat, self-destructs (illegal split) where a teammate can collect the drop,
     ideally next to one. This replaces our 853 kelp deaths and lets us harvest dead ends without
     fear, as they do on Maze and WeakHold.
 I5. Spawn camping. Use the spawn countdowns to send the nearest idle dragon to sit adjacent to
     a tile shortly before it spawns, circling a 2x2 next to it. Turn our idle loops (W5) into
     their spawn loops. Large timed prizes (Tower Defense centre) get several campers in advance.
 I6. Opening factory. Every dragon, the queen included early, splits 2 off at length 4
     immediately; split on the spot when farming a pearl lane so the child inherits the lane. The
     target is their r1-30 tempo (we are 2-7x behind).
 I7. Use free steps. Plan multi-step paths for harvesting and crossing (portals included), and
     keep a share of the fleet at length 5-8 (2 free steps) instead of 2-4.
 I8. Half-split long dragons (about 20+) to stay fast and safe, and "reverse-split" a long dragon
     whose head is boxed in (child head = old tail).
 I9. Long hunters with kamikaze strikes. Hunters of length 5-9 have 5-10 cells of strike reach.
     Shadow the enemy queen just outside her escorts' reach and strike the turn she comes within
     range. Our regicide already works (5 kills); it needs longer hunters and more of them in range.
 I10. Endgame "longest dragon" plan. When the queens are tied (both bunkered) or both dead, pick
     one safe long dragon from about r350 and feed it, as they do (Islands 21 -> 100).

CHANGE
 C1. Queen in combat ("war") mode must stop chasing pearls. Today she keeps the "pearl" target in
     war mode (13 of 16 deaths). In war mode her objective should be: leave enemy sprint reach,
     close to our bodies or the bunker.
 C2. "last-attack" by the queen is only a symptom. Both cases were a length-2 queen already boxed
     in by one stalker. Prevention is I1 plus: never let a stalker hold her at 1-3 cells for
     several rounds; move away or call an interceptor.
 C3. Portal discipline. Claim portal transits (one dragon per portal pair per round) to stop our
     own head-ons through linked portals, and never enter a walled pocket (such as a 2x2 pearl
     box) longer than the pocket can hold.
 C4. Late-game fleet cap. Ours drops to N/32 after round 300 (16 dragons on a 32x16 map); theirs
     holds one per 12-20 cells until about r350 and then converts the extra dragons into length.
     Hold the fleet longer, then convert it (I3, I10) instead of just stopping splits.
 C5. Guard and idle behaviour. Our guards and "keep" dragons circle (W5). Their escorts are few
     (0.3-1.0 within 3 of the queen); safety comes from distance. Put idle dragons on spawn camping
     and feeding instead of ring duty.

REMOVE / STOP
 R1. Stop letting the queen roam for pearls once she is the only thing between us and a win
     (Part 1: 6 games).
 R2. Stop sending length-2 dragons as hunters or interceptors against long targets: reach 1 makes
     them easy to dodge, and they are cheap fodder in even trades.
 R3. Stop "doomed -> walk into kelp" deaths (replace them with I4).

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PART 5. WHERE VIBING++ IS BEATABLE
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 - Their queen self-destructs when boxed in (3 of 17 games). A small net of our dragons that limits
   her free cells could trigger it without a trade.
 - Our regicide killed their queen 5 times, with length 3-7 strikers using 2-5-step orders
   (Islands SWSWS from length 7). Their queen is only safe by distance; longer hunters break that.
 - They do not depend on their queen. After she dies they still win by elimination or longest
   dragon. So after a regicide we must (a) bunker our queen and (b) keep at least one dragon alive
   or out-grow them. Both are cheap if we plan for it.
 - On Portals we out-harvested them 1098 to 614 and owned the map late. Our harvesting can win when
   we take the pearl lanes. Losses there came from the queen tiebreak, not the economy.
 - Head-on trades are even, so a larger fleet wins the attrition. The economy items (I4-I7) are
   what turn even trades into elimination wins for us.

Suggested order, if and when we change code:
 I1 + C1 + I2 (queen survival), then I4 + I5 + I6 (economy), then I3 + I10 (queen feeding and
 the giant), then I7-I9.
