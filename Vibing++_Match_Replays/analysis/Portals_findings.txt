2. HOW VIBING++ PLAYED THIS MAP
 - Central-column production line (r5-r29 in the log): one dragon walks up and down x=15, eating
   the pearls at (15,1),(15,3),(15,5),(15,7) as they respawn. Every time it reaches length 4-5 it
   splits off 2-3 cells right there, and the child continues the same loop: dragons 4 -> 9 -> 10
   -> 11 -> 12 -> 13 -> 14. One pearl lane fed a new dragon every ~3 rounds.
 - From r150 they stopped expanding and converted the fleet into queen length. 187
   self-destructs, 57 of them fed straight to the queen: 57 of her 61 pearls were drops. Their
   fleet went 21 -> 1 while the queen went 4 -> 64.
 - Their queen kept 0.29 own heads within 3 and was never inside our sprint reach (0 of 499 rounds).

3. QUEENS
 - Our queen (length 10) died r266 at (9,9) by moving into a body. In r262-265 she ate 4 pearls,
   using 2-step free moves, and walked into a pocket at (9,9) where every exit was a body. Three
   Vibing++ heads (lengths 3-6) were 2-4 cells away. We were within their sprint reach in 49 rounds
   (19%), the most of any map.

4. WHAT WENT WRONG FOR US (lots of own goals)
 - 60 of our dragons died head-on with each OTHER (30 pairs). Two of our dragons stepped into the
   two ends of a linked portal pair in the same round, e.g. (12,7) S and (19,10) W at r156.
 - 174 "hit itself" deaths, clustered on a few cells such as (12,3) E x13, (5,10) W x12 and
   (9,5) W x10. These are entries into the walled 2x2 pearl boxes through portals; a dragon longer
   than the box coils into itself.
 - 86 wall deaths. Our 16.5% idle 2x2 circling was the highest after SchoolTime and WeakHold.

5. WHAT WENT RIGHT
 - Our economy beat theirs 1098 to 614, and we held most of the map late. This proves our
   harvesting can win when we take the pearl lanes. The game was lost only on the queen tiebreak.

6. TAKEAWAY
 This is the clearest example of their endgame: when the fleet cannot grow, they spend it all on
 the queen. A queen fed to 64 beats a 30-dragon fleet. We need a queen-length plan by r200-300
 and portal discipline (claim portal transits, never enter a box we cannot leave).
