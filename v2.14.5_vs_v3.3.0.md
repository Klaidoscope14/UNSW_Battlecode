## 1. What v2.14.5 was doing

The same fleet architecture as today, as of 2026-10-06 morning:

- **Economy.** Numbers first until a switch round (DYN_ALLOC), then length.
  - Everyone splits at length 4, the queen too, within her own limits.
  - Lane splits, runners on big maps, half-splits at length 20, camping on spawn tiles (future pearls as jobs).
  - Break-even dead-end dives ("mining"); fountains.
  - Greedy Manhattan pearl matching.
  - **REGION_VALUE** (new in v2.14.5): in the opening, explorers head for the map centre (rich ground).
- **Queen.**
  - QUEEN_HOME: a leash of 7 around her spawn, a rest spot, no pockets, few splits (keeps ≥ 3).
  - Hard enemy-reach map (free + paid steps), two-turn lookahead (QUEEN_LOOK2), pursuit penalties (corners, all exits
    in reach).
  - Insurance split when about to be struck; LEAD_SAFE / LATE_SAFE (no pearl trips once ahead or in the last
    rounds).
  - Bunker (trim to 2, circle a 2×2) **only once their queen is known dead**.
- **Army.**
  - Hunters in packs (their queen first, then long dragons) from round 60–100.
  - Guards and screens near our queen; defenders on our half.
  - Snipes and trades only when trading up (TRADE_UP); anti-escort strikes on stalkers.
- **Late game.** Giant from r200 (the longest non-queen dragon grows); heir once their queen is dead; feeding (with
  the FEED_FIX ladder trial on).

Known faults it had (fixed later): the giant / leader explosion (20–30 "leaders" at once on big maps, all not
splitting), and the queen unable to split at length 4 on Queen of Spades.

## 3. Same 32 games, three builds (vs vib2 / swarm2, 8 maps, both sides)

| Build | W-L | Queen deaths | Wins by longer queen / longest dragon / elimination |
|---|---|---|---|
| **v2.14.5** | 31-1 | **9** | **18** / 7 / 6 |
| v3.3.0 with all new switches off | 31-1 | 15 | 11 / 11 / 9 |
| v3.3.0 | 32-0 | 13 | 12 / 9 / 11 |

Locally everything wins, because the sparring bots are weaker than the ladder. The pattern is the useful part:
**v2.14.5 keeps its queen alive and wins on queen length**, while later versions lose the queen more often and win
by longest dragon or elimination instead. Against strong ladder opponents those fallback wins disappear: once our
queen dies we need the longest dragon or an elimination, and good teams beat us at both.

Caution: 32 games is a small sample. On the full 204-game suite v2.14.5 had 43 queen deaths and v3.2.0 36. Locally
there is no version that is clearly worse; the ladder is what punishes the later versions. So the cause has to be
behaviour that our sparring bots don't punish.

## 4. Most likely causes of the ladder drop (behaviour the local suite cannot see)

1. **EQ_INFER (v2.15.2): our queen bunkers when their queen goes unseen for 100 rounds.** *Uncertain, not the top suspect:* the bunker keeps her length, so against teams with a short hidden queen (Stochastic Parrot: length 2-3, never eats) it still wins the tiebreak. It loses only against teams that grow a hidden queen.
   - Locally their queen really is dead in 186 of 204 games, so bunkering is right.
   - Strong ladder opponents keep their queen hidden and alive (the Vibing++ analysis: their queen stays out of
     our reach in 99–100% of rounds).
   - So from about round 150–250 our queen stops eating while theirs grows, and we lose the round-500 "longer queen"
     tiebreak.
   - This exactly matches losing the win type v2.14.5 relied on.
2. **OPEN_PLAN (v2.16.0): no hunters until we have 1.4× their dragons.**
   - Against a strong swarm we may never get that majority, so no hunters for up to 300 rounds and nothing pressures
     their queen.
   - Locally we reach the majority quickly.
3. **VORONOI (v2.15.4): our dragons give way.**
   - Contested pearls drop to 0.35× value, camps are skipped where they arrive first, and explorers keep off their
     side.
   - Against spawn-camping, fast-expanding ladder bots this hands them ground.
   - Locally it improved the protected maps (83-1).
4. **QUEEN_CROWD 2 (flee from 2 heads):** fleeing takes her out of her home area. Helped locally, unproven on the
   ladder.
5. **WALL (big maps):** long collectors march to the middle line; strong teams can pick them off.

Lower risk: GIANT_ONE (fixes a real bug), CHASE / DRY / CAMP limits, SLOT_CHECK, TOPO, LEGAL_BY_VISION, EQ_STATUS_BIT,
FEED_DELIVERY, CHOKE_GUARDS, CORK, the v3.3 fixes.

Also possible: the ladder field got stronger over time. Resubmitting v2.14.5 tests that directly. If it holds about
1700 again, our changes caused the drop; if it falls too, the field moved.
