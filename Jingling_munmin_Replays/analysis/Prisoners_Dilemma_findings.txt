PRISONERS DILEMMA vs Jingling_munmin  (v2.11.0)  -  LOSS by elimination at round 137
Map 32x16 (matches the local map this time: 6 starting dragons). Pearls sit in 1-wide vertical
"prison cell" corridors around the middle. Jingling_munmin started (we are team B).
Raw numbers: analysis/raw/Prisoners_Dilemma_{metrics,deep,econ,profile}.txt

HEADLINE
  Our regicide (#5, length 3) killed their queen at round 34. We had already lost 6 dragons by then
  (2 trapped in cells at round 6, 4 in snipes) and after round 34 our queen was alone. She could not
  enter the prison cells, ate nothing for 87 rounds, and died to a sprinting dragon at round 137.

OPENING (round by round, see opening.py)
  r1  both length-11 starters split. Theirs: 11 -> 2+9 -> 2+7 -> 5+2 (three splits in round 1, keeping
      2-cell heads that go straight into the cells). Ours: 11 -> 6+5, 5 -> 2+3.
  r2-r5 their stubs eat in the cells, split 4 -> 2+2, and the stubs die in the cells (hit itself /
      wall) — recycled by the next dragon.
  r6  two of ours die in cells ("doomed": hit itself, hit a wall).
  r7-r26 five of ours die in snipes (#14, #9, #11, #16, #21), fleet 6/6 -> 3/7.
  By round 30 they had eaten ~22 pearls, we had eaten 7.

WHAT JINGLING_MUNMIN DOES
  - Cell mining: 27 "hit a wall" + 25 "hit itself" deaths, almost all with a free cell (chosen).
  - Splits 4 -> 2+2 (24) and 5 -> 2+3 (31). Max fleet only 8 — this map limits everyone.
  - Their head was within 1 of a spawn tile 97% of their turns (ours 44%).

WHERE WE LACKED
  A. Snipes when even or behind: 5 snipe deaths in the first 26 rounds. They are what left the queen
     alone.
  B. The lone queen. Length 3, "pearl" 100% of her status texts after round 50, ate 0 pearls in 87
     dragon-turns. All pearls were in 1-wide dead-end cells, and the v2.7 loop rule (a queen of
     length <= 3 must always be able to reach a free 2x2 loop) forbids entering them. She hovered in
     the empty open area. She could not split (needs length 4).
  C. Their queen's death was seen only by the striker, who died in the trade -> no bunker; our queen
     kept "pearl" in war mode near their dragons and was hit by a sprinting L3-5 at round 137.

WHAT WOULD HAVE CHANGED THE RESULT
  - No snipes in the opening -> 5 more eaters/blockers alive.
  - A starving lone queen should be allowed into safe-looking cells (or should hide in a corner of
    the open area rather than hover near their dragons) — their queen was dead, we only had to live.
