WEAKHOLD vs Stochastic Parrot  (fleet-v2.13.2)  -  LOSS by elimination at round 443
Map 40x15, a kelp maze. 22 "fountain" tiles refill 1 round after being eaten, all inside 1-wide
dead-end pockets: (11-13,0) (13-15,14) (7-8,5) (8,6) (7-8,9) on our half and the mirror images on
theirs. Most other tiles spawn every 100 or 500 rounds. We moved first (team A).
Raw numbers: analysis/raw/Weakhold_{metrics,deep,econ,profile,tactics}.txt

HEADLINE
  An economic deadlock. From r20 to r250 we had exactly 4 dragons of length 2 (total length 8) and ate
  no pearls at all between r75 and r200, while they mined the fountains and grew from 4 to 45 dragons.
  The queen died at r262 (pinned along a corridor), and the last dragon at r443.

THE DEADLOCK (simulated and reproduced locally)
  - Our dragons sat next to the fountain pockets in 2x2 loops for up to 190 rounds. Example: #20
    circled (9-10, 0-1) from r45 to r235, next to the pocket (11-13,0), which held 3 pearls the whole
    time (the pearls appeared at r44-45 and were next eaten at r235).
  - Early on (r9-r43) we did mine that pocket 4 times, but always with the losing pattern: enter at
    length 2, eat 2, "grow-fleet" split 2+2 inside the pocket, the head part eats the third and dies at
    length 3. Net length per trip about 0 (14 trips, +3 in total).
  - The queen and the guard looped by the bottom pocket (13-15,14); the 4th dragon's target flipped
    between pearls in the centre box (planner distance 9-18 vs true 15-20 through the maze).
  - Pearls next to our heads at the start of a round were left uneaten 152 of 203 times.
  - Local check: v2.13.2 vs vib2 on weakhold, 3 seeds: total length 8, 8, 10 at r500 (vib2 dies on its
    own, so the bot "wins" locally and the stall was never visible). v2.12 stalls the same way;
    switching off CAMP_JOB or POCKET_CHECK, or lowering DIVE_MIN_GAIN or STALL_ROUNDS, does not help.
    A debug build shows the dive value at the pocket mouth is 1.00 when the dragon enters (exactly
    the threshold) and 0.0-0.5 at the bottom pocket, where its pearl list was missing two of the three
    pocket pearls.

WHAT STOCHASTIC PARROT DOES HERE
  - Fountain mining at scale: 155 pocket trips; 435 of their 761 pearls (57%) came from the fountains.
  - Their trip: a length 2-4 dragon walks in, eats all 3, splits off everything but 2 at the bottom
    (child 3-4 cells walks out), and the 2-cell stub dies (114 wall hits, 41 self-hits). Net +0.77
    length per trip (+119 in total), and the stub's drop is eaten by the next miner (125 own drops
    re-eaten within 3 rounds). 312 splits, child sizes 2/3/4.
  - They took 214 pearls on our half; we took 0 on theirs.
  - Their queen: length 2 after the first split, 0 pearls eaten, median 4 cells from spawn.

HOW OUR QUEEN DIED (r262)
  She ran east along row 0 eating pearls (L2 -> L4) toward the (11-13,0) pocket. Enemy #130 (L2) came
  up column 4 and then ran east along row 1, parallel and behind her. At r261 she turned south into
  (10,1), a cell with kelp east and south, so its only exit was back west into the corridor #130 was
  running along. #130 arrived next round: head-on.

TAKEAWAY FOR THIS MAP
  This map is won by fountain mining. Our bot has no notion of a renewable pocket, splits in the
  wrong place inside it, and lets the fleet idle for 200 rounds.
