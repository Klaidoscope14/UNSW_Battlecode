HEADLINE
  The log header says "elimination", but 13 of our dragons (total length 49) were still alive at
  round 500. The game was decided by the queen rule. Our queen died at round 271 (length 2, stalked

MAP ECONOMY (important for unseen maps)
  186 of the 196 pearl tiles are "once per game" tiles (they spawn near round 1 and practically never
  again). The only renewable food is 10 "fountain" tiles that refill 1-5 rounds after being eaten:
  (18,0) (19,0) (18,1) (35,0) (35,1) (18,16) (18,17) (35,16) (34,17) (35,17), each in a tiny
  dead-end pocket at the foot of the kelp columns, plus death drops.
  After the opening grab, whoever farms the fountains grows.

WHERE WE LACKED
  A. Fountains. Their fountain pearls per 100 rounds: 17, 32, 38, 39, 33 (159 in total = 36% of their
     pearls). Ours: 39, 23, then 0 after round 200.
     Their trip: enter at length 2-3, eat all, split off everything but 2 at the bottom
     (child 3-4 gets out), the 2-cell stub dies (39 walls, 17 self-hits). Net +0.98 length per trip
     over 56 trips, and the stub's drop feeds the next miner.
     Our trip: enter at length 2, eat 2, "grow-fleet" split 2+2 inside the pocket, the head part eats
     the third and dies at length 3 (21 of 25 stubs were length 3). Net +0.12 per trip over 25 trips.
     These are the "pearl L3 / grow-fleet L4 / pearl L2 -> hit a wall, age ~11" deaths (26 "doomed"
     wall deaths).
  B. Queen. Length 2-4 all game; roamed (median 12 cells from start, max 31); an enemy head within 4
     cells in 39% of rounds; status "war pearl" among the swarm.
     #117 (L2) stayed exactly 2 cells (diagonal) from her for 15 rounds (r256-270), mirroring each
     turn, and struck when her move and its move met on (29,9) at r271.
     Their queen: length 3 all game, 0 pearls eaten, median 4 cells from spawn, an enemy head within 4
     in only 4% of rounds.
  C. Snipes. 18 "snipe" deaths and 1 "attack", all 1-for-1 trades for their L2-L4 dragons, 18 of 19
     while they had more dragons (8-14 of ours vs 12-25). The same undercount as on Australia.
  D. Fleet stuck at 7-16 dragons all game (theirs reached 53).

WHAT STOCHASTIC PARROT DOES HERE
  - Fountain mining with stub-2 splits (above), continuously from r0 to r500.
  - Ate 175 pearls on our half (we ate 21 on theirs).
  - Many own losses: 48 walls, 25 self-hits, 22 body hits, 42 head-ons; does not matter to them.
  - Queen hidden near spawn, never eats; queen-seekers for ours (approach lift 2.0 at distance 2).

TAKEAWAY FOR THIS MAP
  Mine the fountains properly (enter long enough, split at the bottom keeping 2, keep a miner on
  each pocket), and keep the queen at home: she needed only length 4 at r500.
