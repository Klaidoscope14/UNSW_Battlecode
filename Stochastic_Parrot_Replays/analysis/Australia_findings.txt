  Length 4 -> 3 at the start, then 3 for the whole game; ate 1 pearl in 500 rounds; stayed within 3

WHERE WE LACKED
  A. Queen safety while ahead (the decisive error; see above). Two consecutive moves into kelp-corner
     cells (2 and 1 open sides) with an enemy 3 cells away. Her survival check treats enemy heads as
     still; it did not foresee the L2 closing the single exit.
  B. Self-chosen trades. 80 of our 225 deaths were optional attack moves (57 "snipe", 18 "attack",
     2 "hunt", 2 "intercept", 1 "pack-strike"; plus 2 forced "last-attack"). Every one was a 1-for-1
     head-on, mostly against their L2-L5 dragons. 78 of the 80 were made while they had more dragons
     (often 2.4x: 27 vs 64).
     v2.12's SAFE_TRADES should have blocked them; it did not because "outnumbered" compares our true
     unit count with the number of distinct enemy ids we heard of in 60 rounds (a handful of their
     64), so every check passed. Their dragons do not attack ours (approach lift 0.75-0.94), so all
     80 of these deaths were avoidable.
  C. Self-inflicted deaths: 70 "doomed" deaths (26 wall, 23 body, 21 self), 77 of 101 non-combat
     deaths with the head on a corridor cell; 61 dragons died within 10 rounds of birth.
     Split children dying within 2 rounds: 13 of 20 "unwind-split" children and 12 of 43
     "save-split" children.
  D. Fleet growth. Equal at r75 (19/19); they hit the 64 cap at r150, we peaked at 47. Their splits
     r50-199: 108 vs our 83, while we lost 62 dragons in r50-199
     (25 head-ons, 19 walls, 12 self, 6 bodies).

WHAT STOCHASTIC PARROT DOES HERE
  - Swarm of L2-L4 dragons; 261 of 283 splits leave a child of 2; fleet capped at 64 from r150-400.
  - Spawn presence: head adjacent at 22% of spawns (us 14%); they won 708 races where they were
    nearer (we won 501 where we were nearer, and lost 82 where we were nearer).
  - Hidden, never-eating L3 queen near spawn (see above). Queen-seekers go for our queen.
  - Endgame: fleet 64 -> 22 in r400-500 (33 "hit itself" deaths in that window = recycling) while one
    dragon grows 16 -> 42 (the longest at the end). Same consolidation as Byte_Force's giant.
  - They lost 270 dragons (128 head-ons, 67 self, 42 walls) and did not care: production > losses.

TAKEAWAY FOR THIS MAP
  Keep the queen alive and above their queen's length (theirs is always 2-4) and we win at r500.
  Stop optional trades. A queen ahead on length should stop pearl trips into the coastline.
