2. STOCHASTIC PARROT'S APPROACH (reverse-engineered)
=====================================================================================================
  S1. A hidden, starving queen. She splits once at the start (to length 2-3) and then eats
      0-2 pearls per game. She stays near her spawn (median 3-4 cells, max 9-12) and loops there.
      Our heads came within 4 cells of her in 0-7% of rounds (Default excepted). She never needs to
      be long: they win by elimination or by killing our queen.
  S2. Queen-seekers and escorts. Their dragons ignore ours (approach lift 0.9-1.1 at 3-5 cells; they
      back off at 2 cells, lift 0.5-0.9) but move toward our queen 1.5-2.0x more often than chance.
      Typical kill: an L2 runs beside our queen's head (1 cell, Prisoners, 14 rounds) or diagonally
      (2 cells, Autarky, 15 rounds), parallel to a kelp wall, until she runs out of exits; or it
      walks straight at her when she enters a corner (Australia, Weakhold).
  S3. Split churn. 4 -> 2+2 almost always (Default 204/207, Prisoners 82/91 splits); 40-64 dragons.
  S4. Fountain mining. Tiles that refill 1-5 rounds after being eaten sit in 1-wide dead-end pockets
      on Weakhold (22), Autarky (10) and Prisoners (8). A dragon of length 2-4 walks in, eats all,
      splits off everything but 2 at the bottom (child walks out), and the 2-cell stub dies (wall or
      own neck); the next miner eats the stub's drop. Weakhold: 155 trips, 435 of 761 pearls, net
      +0.77 length per trip. Autarky: 56 trips, +0.98 per trip.
  S5. Spawn presence. Their head is next to the spawn tile at 21-48% of spawns (ours 1-14%); nearest
      head at a spawn median 2-4 cells (ours 6-15).
  S6. Losses are irrelevant to them. 59-286 deaths per game: friendly head-ons (Default ~67),
      walls, self-hits, own bodies. 40-232 own drops re-eaten within 3 rounds.
  S7. Endgame consolidation on big maps (Australia): fleet 64 -> 22 in the last 100 rounds
      (33 "hit itself" = recycling), longest dragon 16 -> 42.
  S8. No real attack on our ordinary dragons. Nearly all our head-on deaths were moves we chose
      (attacks/snipes) or pearl races.

3. WHERE WE LACKED (ranked by games affected)
=====================================================================================================
  L1. QUEEN SAFETY (5/5 games).
      Our queen roams (median 6-14 cells from spawn, max 12-31) for pearls, an enemy head within 4
      cells in 8-42% of rounds; theirs 0-7% (except Default, where she roamed and died).
      Her survival check treats enemy heads as standing still, which is exactly what escorts exploit:
        - Prisoners r243-257: pinned between a kelp wall and a parallel L2 for 14 rounds, boxed at
          the top corner, "last-attack".
        - Autarky r256-271: an L2 at 2 cells for 15 rounds, struck when their moves met.
        - Weakhold r261: turned into a dead-end cell with an L2 running down the corridor 2 cells away.
        - Australia r371-372 (L7, well ahead): stepped into a 2-sided cell, then a 2-step free sprint
          into a 1-exit kelp corner with an L2 3 cells away.
        - Default r79: L2 queen walked toward two L3s (6 -> 2 cells) while pearl-hunting.
      She was ahead on queen length on Australia for 65 rounds and still went pearl-hunting
      (LATE_SAFE starts only 40 rounds from the end; TIE_BUNKER only for length <= 3).
  L2. OPTIONAL TRADES ON A FALSE "NOT OUTNUMBERED" (4/5 games; 115 of our deaths).
      Optional attack deaths: Australia 80 (57 snipes), Autarky 19, Default 14, Prisoners 2 - every one
      a 1-for-1 head-on, mostly for their L2-L3 dragons, 108 of 115 while they really had more dragons.
      SAFE_TRADES / HUNT_GATE / snipe use distinctEnemies = enemy ids heard in the last 60 rounds,

  L3. NO FOUNTAIN ECONOMY (3/5 games: Weakhold, Autarky, Prisoners).
      Our pocket trips split 2+2 halfway in ("grow-fleet"), so the head part dies at length 3: net
      0.0-0.2 length per trip (theirs +0.8-1.0). Trip counts: 14 vs 155 (Weakhold), 25 vs 56 (Autarky).
      On Weakhold our 4 dragons sat beside full pockets for up to 190 rounds (reproduced locally:
      v2.13.2 and v2.12 both stay at total length 8-10 for 500 rounds on weakhold; vib2 dies on its

  L4. LOW-FLEET DEATH SPIRAL (3/5: Prisoners, Weakhold, Default).

4. WHAT TO INTEGRATE / CHANGE / REMOVE (proposals only - nothing implemented)
=====================================================================================================
  Win condition against this style: their queen is always length 2-4, so a living queen of length
  >= 5 at r500 wins. Everything below serves that, plus not being eliminated.

  INTEGRATE (new)
  I1. Home-anchored queen (copy S1). Choose a home area near our spawn in open water (cells with 3+
      open sides, no portals, no dead ends or 1-wide corridors nearby) and keep her within a leash of
      about 4-6 cells. Pearls only inside the leash and only with no enemy head within 6. She grows
      late through feeders (existing feed logic), not by roaming. [fixes L1 on all maps]
  I2. Pursuer-aware queen moves. Treat each enemy head within ~5 as moving: after her candidate move,
      can enemy heads reach all her next exits within their reach? Never end a move (including free
      sprint steps) on a cell with <= 2 open sides while an enemy head is within 4. [Australia,
      Weakhold]
  I3. Anti-escort reflex. An enemy head within 2 of her for 3 rounds running = a stalker:
      (a) she turns away from kelp walls toward open water instead of running along them;
      (b) the nearest 1-2 teammates are called (sonar) to intercept it - a 1-for-1 trade for a stalker
          is always worth it and must be exempt from the trade gate;
      (c) a queen of length >= 4 uses her stall/split options before reaching the corner, not at it.
      [Prisoners, Autarky]
  I4. Lead protection. Once her length exceeds their queen's last known length (or >= 5 from r300 if
      theirs is unknown), she stops optional pearl trips and loops in a safe ring (QUEEN_LOOP_SAFE
      ring, bunker guards), well before the last 40 rounds. [Australia]
  I5. Fountain mining (map-independent: the bot sees each tile's pearl countdown). A tile whose
      countdown resets to <= 5 after being eaten is a fountain; if it lies in a dead-end pocket:
        - one miner per pocket at a time (a queue), entering at length >= 2;
        - no growth split while inside a dead end; at the bottom split off all but 2;
        - value a trip as pearls + the stub's drop recaptured by the next miner - 2;
        - when fountains are the main renewable food (few other short-period tiles), mining is the
          default job for spare dragons.
      [Weakhold, Autarky, Prisoners]
  I6. An enemy count that cannot undercount. For example: assume outnumbered unless the distinct
      enemies seen are already >= our units; or scale the enemies seen by the share of the map our
      fleet currently sees. [L2]

  CHANGE (existing behaviour)
  C1. Trades: only trade up - their queen, a dragon at least twice our length, or a stalker of our
      queen (I3). Their dragons do not attack ours, so passivity costs nothing against this style.
      [115 deaths]
  C2. Survival mode = safe economy, not no economy: keep eating uncontested pearls and mining
      fountains, keep splitting back up to a floor (about 6-8); never step onto a pearl cell an enemy
      head can reach next turn; no RECYCLE while units <= 5. [Prisoners, Weakhold, Default]
  C3. Opening: until about r100, or while not ahead in dragons, no hunt-queen, guard, screen or
      guard-line jobs - every non-queen farms (the queen hides at home per I1, so she needs fewer
      guards). Avoid even brawls at the start (Prisoners centre). [Default, Prisoners]
  C4. Stall detection in mazes: a dragon in a 2x2 loop for ~8 rounds without eating while it targets
      a pearl has stalled - blacklist that target (Weakhold: planner distances through unseen edges
      were 9 vs a true 15-20, and the target flipped). [Weakhold]
  C5. Survival splits: check the child can escape before "unwind-split" and "save-split" (7/20 and
      31/43 children survived 2 rounds on Australia). Low priority.
  C6. Hunting: keep opportunistic regicide and the cheap shadow (one dragon 2 cells from a roaming
      enemy queen - it worked on Default); drop long hunter trips toward a queen hidden in a home
      full of their swarm (average 12-41 cells away, never reached).

  REMOVE
  R1. SNIPE as an even-trade tool (57 deaths on Australia alone); keep it only for C1 targets.
  R2. RECYCLE in small fleets.
  (QUEEN_INSURE: neutral - it fired once, when she was already boxed.)

=====================================================================================================
5. CONSISTENCY WITH THE EARLIER LADDER ANALYSIS (Byte_Force / Jingling_munmin, v2.11)
=====================================================================================================
  Same lessons, stronger now: survival beats offence; optional trades lose to swarms; numbers are
  built by recycling and pocket mining. New with Stochastic Parrot: their queen does not die by
  herself (it hides and never eats), and they hunt our queen with escorts. So against this style
  OUR queen's survival is the whole game, and fountain maps need a real mining routine.
