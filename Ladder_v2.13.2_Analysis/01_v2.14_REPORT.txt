fleet-v2.14.0 - the Stochastic Parrot fixes: what changed, how it was tested
Built on v2.13.2. Every item has its own switch at the top of main.cpp (section "v2.14").

QUEEN SAFETY (findings L1, proposals I1-I4)
  QUEEN_HOME      The queen keeps to a leash of 7 cells round her spawn. She goes only for pearls
                  inside it, with no enemy head within 5, and never into a pocket (1-wide dead end).
                  With nothing safe to eat she waits at a "resting spot": the most open known water
                  inside the leash. Teammates leave pearls within 3 cells of her to her.
                  She never steps into a pocket (directional dead-end test). She splits only while
                  the fleet has fewer than 8 dragons, keeps 2 real exits, and never below length 4.
                  Their hidden queens are 2-3 long, so ours stays ahead on the tiebreak.
                  Feeders that have no fresh report of her walk to her home, and a short (<= 3) or
                  unknown queen counts as "behind" from r300, so feeding starts earlier.
  PURSUIT_SAFE    Enemy heads move too:
                    - a cell with only one way on costs CORNER_PEN when any dragon is near (a fifth
                      of it otherwise);
                    - all ways on within an enemy's next two steps costs ALL_EXITS_PEN;
                    - stepping into a 1-wide corridor costs CORR_ENTER_PEN, more with another dragon
                      within 6;
                    - near an enemy, more free cells score higher (open water over walls);
                    - her free-step sprints never end in a corner with an enemy within 5
                      (the Australia death).
  ANTI_ESCORT     An enemy head within 2 of our queen for 2+ rounds is a stalker.
                    - The queen reports it (danger + enemy report, high priority).
                    - Short teammates strike it (the trade gate does not apply).
                    - Teammates within 12 come to the reported cell ("rescue").
  LEAD_SAFE       From r300, a queen longer than theirs (or >= 5 with theirs unknown) makes no pearl
                  trips (the last-40-rounds rule, earlier).

TRADES (L2, I6, C1)
  ENEMY_EST       The trade gates use an enemy count scaled by how much of the map our fleet watches
                  (40 cells per dragon, at least 25% coverage). Before, this was the number of ids we
                  had heard of, which said "27 vs 6" when it was 27 vs 64.
  TRADE_UP        While outnumbered (by that count) or in survival mode, head-ons, snipes and hunts
                  are only for their queen, a dragon at least 2x our length, or a stalker. When we are
                  not outnumbered, even trades stay allowed (an opponent that attacks our dragons must
                  be answered: local tests vs fleet11).

ECONOMY (L3-L5, I5, C2-C4, C6)
  FOUNTAINS       Tiles that refill within 3 rounds are detected from the tile countdown (it stays at
                  1). The dive search counts them as pearls. No optional split while inside a dead
                  end: the miner eats to the bottom and the survival split keeps 2 (the opponent's
                  pattern). One miner per pocket; fountain pearls are worth 1.2x.
  SURV_ECON       Survival mode: stepping next to an enemy head for a pearl costs 200k (was 9k); no
                  recycling below 6 dragons.
  OPEN_FARM_ROUND Before r100: no guards, screens, hunters or long queen chases. GUARD_FLEET_MIN 3 -> 5.
  LOOP_STALL      Circling one 2x2 for 8 rounds without eating while after a pearl more than 3 away:
                  that pearl is dropped for 30 rounds.
  HUNT_FAR_SMALL  On maps above 1200 cells, packs only go for their queen when she was seen in the
                  last 30 rounds (no long walks to a guessed home full of their swarm).

TESTS (short, as agreed)
  Weakhold (the deadlock map), local:
    - Before: total length 8-10 for 500 rounds.
    - Now: 85 pocket trips per game, every stub 2 cells, +0.74 length per trip, 573 pearls (was 43).
    - Weakhold vs vib2/swarm2, both sides: all wins (v2.13.2 lost to swarm2 there).
  84-game regression (vib2, swarm2, fleet11 x 14 maps x 2 sides):
      v2.13.2      77-7, queen deaths 24
      v2.14.0      78-6, queen deaths 17 (vib2 27-1, swarm2 27-1, fleet11 24-4)
      (trade-up even when not outnumbered: 79-5, 17 - same within noise; shipped the adaptive one)
  Problems found and fixed during testing:
    - The queen walked into a fountain pocket after a split.
    - Teammates and her own children boxed in the home queen in corridors.
    - With no target she drifted along walls into corners.
    - She starved at length 2 because her leash held only pockets; this is why the queen now keeps
      length >= 4.
  Smoke test (quick.sh): 9-1, peak CPU 25.1M points per turn; the zip itself from a clean folder in the
  sandbox (unsw): win, peak 26.1M.
  Still open: portals side A (lost by v2.13.2 too); stripes vs fleet11 (old weakness).
  The real test is the ladder - especially against Stochastic Parrot.
