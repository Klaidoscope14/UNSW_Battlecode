TOWER DEFENSE - 13 losses (12 unique: 1705 and 1705-Ciallo are the same file), fleet-v2.14.0
Loss types: elimination 5, longest dragon 5, longer queen 2.
Raw: Tower_Defense/analysis/raw/*, master table: analysis/master.csv

WHAT HAPPENS
  Both sides start with 2 dragons of length 2 and almost no pearls until the centre starts spawning
  around round 100. Up to r100 it is level (3 vs 3 dragons). From r100 to r150 they go 6 -> 29
  dragons and eat 105 pearls; we go 3 -> 4 and eat 24 (game 1525). Medians over the 12 games:
  dragons at r100 3 vs 9.5, at r300 4 vs 32; pearls 65 vs 516.

ROOT CAUSE (proved by local ablation, same seeds and opponents)
  v2.14's "no optional split when every move looks like a trap" rule (part of FOUNTAINS: calm =
  !inDeadEnd, inDeadEnd = !anySafe) fires all over Tower Defense's corridor maze, so our dragons stop
  splitting exactly when the centre opens.
    v2.13.2            our pearls by r150 137, dragons at r150 30.5
    v2.14.0            our pearls by r150  15.5, dragons at r150 5
    v2.14.0 without that one rule        101.5 pearls, 23.5 dragons
  (Weakhold mining does not need the rule: without it Weakhold is unchanged.)

QUEENS
  Their queen died first in 9 of 12 games (median r122, mostly self-inflicted); ours died later in 10
  (median r367, length 2 after survival splits). A queen that simply survived would have won 9 games
  on the queen rule. 2 losses on queen length (ours 12 vs 14 and 12 vs 20).

OPPONENTS: miga bot, Quantify, Akatsuki, StarStruck, Ciallo, Machli and 5 unnamed - swarm (2+2 splits).
