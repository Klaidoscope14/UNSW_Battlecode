COMBINED FINDINGS - 52 ladder losses of fleet-v2.14.0 on Trophy, Stripes, Islands, Trauma,
Tower Defense and Weakhold. Observations and proposals only: nothing has been merged into the bot.

Per-map files: Replays/analysis/<Map>_findings.txt
Data: Replays/analysis/master.csv (one row per game, 95 columns), 00_ingest.txt (checks),
      02_queen_deaths.txt (every queen death), 03_queen_lastmove.txt (her options before each head-on),
      Replays/<Map>/analysis/raw/* and SCORECARD.txt (per game, from run_all.sh + tactics.sh)

=====================================================================================================
0. DATA
=====================================================================================================
  52 files -> 49 unique games. Elo-1626 (Trophy) has only its header; Islands 1494 = 1494-migabot and
  Tower Defense 1705 = 1705-Ciallo are byte-identical. Three headers named the wrong starter (Islands
  1493, Trophy 1595, Stripes 1635); the parser now finds our side from our own "QUEEN" status lines.
  Every game replays cleanly (no impossible moves; wall deaths sit on real kelp).
  How we lost (from the end state):
    longest dragon 21 (both queens dead), elimination 20, longer queen 8 (both alive, ours shorter).

=====================================================================================================
1. THE FIVE CAUSES (ranked by losses explained)
=====================================================================================================
  C1. OUR QUEEN DOES NOT SURVIVE - 42 of 49 games; decisive in 29.
      Their queen died FIRST in 29 games (median r81-122; 11 killed by our dragons, the rest by their own
      mistakes). Ours was alive then and died later (median r170-230). The rule is "longer living queen
      wins", so a queen that only survived would have won all 29. The bot cannot know their queen is
      dead (no such API, and detection needs a witness), so the queen must be safe all game.
      How she dies (42 deaths): 33 head-ons, 9 boxed in. Of the 33 head-ons:
        -  9: a move outside every enemy's reach AND with 2+ exits existed in the fatal round;
        - 22: none that round, but one existed the round before (a two-move lookahead would have
              saved her);
        -  6: trapped two rounds earlier.
      Killers: ordinary L2-L5 enemy dragons 2-3 cells away for 1-5 rounds, half of them sprinting
      (free + paid steps). She was alone (no teammate within 3) in most cases. Status at death: pearl 15,
      rest 11 (the v2.14 resting spot), boxed 9, insurance split 4, bunker 1.
      Gap in v2.14's pursuit rule: "all exits within an enemy's next two steps" uses free steps only;
      the killers use paid steps.

  C2. THE OPENING IS TOO SLOW - decides the 20 eliminations; feeds the rest.
      Pearls by r100 (median): Stripes 11 vs 41, Tower Defense 4.5 vs 23.5, Trophy 31 vs 141, Weakhold
      6 vs 51. Dragons at r100: Stripes 1 vs 13.5, Tower Defense 3 vs 9.5, Trophy 10 vs 32, Weakhold 3 vs 14.
      Local A/B with the same seeds and opponents shows this is a v2.14 REGRESSION, not the opponents:
                           our pearls by r100        dragons at r150
                           v2.13.2   v2.14.0         v2.13.2  v2.14.0
        Tower Defense        42        8.5             30.5      5
        Trophy              126       55.5
        Islands             198      151.5
        Stripes              17       10
      Switch ablation (v2.14.0 with one switch off):
        - Tower Defense: the "no optional split when every move looks like a trap" rule (part of
          FOUNTAINS) stops all splitting in its corridor maze. Without that one rule: 101.5 pearls by
          r150 (was 15.5). Weakhold mining does not need it.
        - QUEEN_HOME (leash 7, resting spot, no queen split below length 8): without it, our pearls by
          r150 rise on every weak map: Islands 302->353, Stripes 14.5->42, Trauma 7->25.5, Weakhold
          20->49, Trophy (by r100) 55->109. On 2-dragon starts (Stripes, Tower Defense) the queen is
          half our fleet and she was resting 17-63% of the opening.
        - PURSUIT_SAFE: halves the Trophy opening (55 -> 100 without it); little effect elsewhere.

  C3. OUR QUEEN IS TOO SHORT WHEN BOTH SURVIVE - 8 games (Trauma 5, Tower Defense 2, Islands 1).
      Their queens reached 11-28 (they roam and eat 19-36 pearls); ours 6-12. On Trauma we won the
      economy outright (e.g. 642 pearls vs 72) but our queen ate 1-6 pearls in 500 rounds. She was never
      threatened (enemies within 4 in 0-3% of rounds): the leash starved her. v2.14 assumed enemy queens
      stay at 2-3 (true for Stripes-type swarms, false for Verity, StarStruck, Machli, NeungzAI, BitShift).
      Feeding is broken: 0 feeding sacrifices in all 49 games; "walking to feed" 0.09% of dragon-turns,
      almost all after r450.

  C4. NO GIANT FOR THE LONGEST-DRAGON TIEBREAK - 21 games.
      When both queens are dead the longest dragon wins. Their longest at the end: median 22 (up to 38),
      first 5+ longer than ours around r225; ours median 11. On Islands our queen WAS our longest dragon
      (13-34 at death), so her death also lost us the longest-dragon tiebreak.

  C5. POCKET TRIPS INTO PORTAL BOXES LOSE EVERYTHING (Stripes).
      Theirs: enter a 2x2 portal box at length 2-3, eat 1-2, walk back out through the portal while still
      short enough to turn (75-90% of trips), +0.9 to +1.3 length per trip. Ours: enter at 3-4, eat to
      5-6, get stuck, survival split, the front 2 cells die: net 0 per trip. (Weakhold-style 1-wide
      fountain pockets are fine: there we now match them at +0.9 per trip.)

=====================================================================================================
2. THE OPPONENTS
=====================================================================================================
  None of them hunt our ordinary dragons (approach lift ~1.0); most drift toward our queen (1.0-1.6).
  A. Swarm, disposable queen (Quantify, miga bot, Ravenus, Manny, Outsider Traders, Jet2Holiday, Hydra):
     2+2 splits from round ~13, 10-47 dragons by r100, queen ~4 long and often dead by r100, heavy
     pocket mining, win by elimination or a late giant.
  B. Queen growers (Verity, StarStruck, Machli, NeungzAI, BitShift, Niuniu Lai): queen roams, eats and
     reaches 14-28; they win on queen length.
  C. Giant builders (Akatsuki 30, Ciallo 19, Verity): one long dragon late in the game.

=====================================================================================================
3. WHAT V2.14 DID (verdict per switch, from these games + the local ablation)
=====================================================================================================
  FOUNTAINS - dive counts fountains   HELPED (Weakhold mining now equals theirs). Keep.
  FOUNTAINS - no split in "dead end"  HURT badly (Tower Defense collapse). Remove or restrict to real
                                      1-wide dead ends.
  QUEEN_HOME                          HURT the opening on every weak map and starved the queen on
                                      Trauma; did NOT keep her alive (11 deaths while resting).
  PURSUIT_SAFE                        Mixed: hurts the Trophy opening; its two-step rule misses paid steps.
  ENEMY_EST / TRADE_UP                Neutral-to-good (few attack deaths except Islands/Trophy, where
                                      the adaptive "not outnumbered" lets even trades through).
  ANTI_ESCORT                         Never fired (stalker strike 0, rescue 0.01%). Harmless.
  LEAD_SAFE / bunker                  Bunker reached in 1 game of 49; late-safe in 8.
  LOOP_STALL, SURV_ECON, OPEN_FARM    No measurable effect here.

=====================================================================================================
4. PROPOSALS (for your decision; each behind its own switch, nothing keyed to map names)
=====================================================================================================
  P1. Remove the "no optional split when !anySafe" rule, or apply it only when every legal move leads
      into a real 1-wide dead end. Keep the fountain dive.            [C2: Tower Defense]
  P2. Make the home queen adaptive instead of fixed:
        - opening: the queen splits and eats like before (back to v2.13.2 behaviour until the fleet is
          established, e.g. 6+ dragons);
        - leash grows when no enemy head has been seen or reported near her for a while, and shrinks
          to home when enemies appear;
        - no idle "rest" while nothing threatens her.                [C2, C3]
  P3. Queen two-move safety: score each move by the margin to every enemy's reach over two turns,
      counting paid steps (free + L-2), and prefer moves after which a safe, open continuation exists.
      A reachable cell is never taken over a safe one with 2+ exits; corner penalties never outweigh
      being inside an enemy's reach.                                  [C1: 31 of 33 head-on deaths]
  P4. Queen length: grow her toward their known/estimated queen length + margin when she is safe
      (she eats herself inside her safe area), and repair feeding so sacrifices actually happen next to
      her (they never did in 49 games).                                [C3]
  P5. Giant insurance: from ~r200, keep one long non-queen dragon (the heir) safe and growing so that
      if both queens die we still win the longest-dragon tiebreak; never let the queen be our only long
      dragon.                                                          [C4]
  P6. Portal-box pockets: enter only at length <= 3 and leave through the portal before reaching 4,
      instead of eating to the bottom.                                 [C5]

  P5  low    - existing GIANT/HEIR logic, extended earlier and to all maps.
  P6  low    - only applies to portal boxes.
  Acceptance before shipping: the usual 84-game regression + the protected maps on both sides against
