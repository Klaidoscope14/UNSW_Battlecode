PLAN v2.13 - finish the partial items, build the missing ones (plan only; nothing implemented yet)
Built on v2.12.0. Every change gets its own switch. Rules that come from the ladder lessons stay in
force: no trades while outnumbered / in survival mode (SAFE_TRADES, HUNT_GATE), numbers first on
congested maps, length on open maps.

Facts checked in the code / engine before planning
  F1. Sonar echoes are totals per turn (kelp, ally, ally head, enemy, enemy head) - no direction and
      no distance. The queen cannot use her own pings as radar; item 3 can only improve by geometry.
  F2. A pearl (almost) never appears on a tile a living dragon covers: 35 of ~4,700 spawns in 3 local
      games, probably tails leaving that round. A camper must wait BESIDE the tile, never on it.
  F3. queen_can_survive() is generic (time-aware body, own-body crossings, depth ~2L+6, node cap).
      It can be the pocket test for any dragon.
  F4. In the flood test, a known portal counts as open water on the far side and an unknown portal as
      a wall, so a 2x2 portal box looks roomy (the room is counted beyond its other portals).
  F5. Gap left in v2.12: the generic "hunt" block (combat-role dragons chasing long enemies) and the
      14-cell "hunt-queen" chase are not covered by HUNT_GATE.
  F6. tools/replay_to_txt.mjs already converts local .replay files to text logs.

PHASE A - defence and economy (low risk)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A0. Close the v2.12 gap (F5).  "hunt" and the long "hunt-queen" chase only when not outnumbered and
    not in survival mode; a reported queen within 6 cells is still chased (a regicide is always
    worth it).

A1. Item 21 - pocket size check (partial -> full).
    Now: generic flood check; portal boxes look roomy (F4).
    Change: for a non-queen move that crosses a portal, or whose destination region is enclosed and
    small (no unexplored edge, area < 3x length), run queen_can_survive() on the body after the move.
    If it fails, the move counts as a trap, unless it is an accepted pocket-mining dive (v2.12: a
    teammate within 5 to eat the stub's drop). Blind portal crossings (far side unknown) only for
    dragons of length <= 3, which can always turn round in a 2x2 box.
    Check: Portals/Stripes/QoS replays - fewer "doomed" deaths inside boxes.

A2. Item 12 - spawn camping as a job (partial -> full).
    Now: future spawns are pearl candidates (exact timing conf 0.85) in the greedy matching; idle
    dragons camp; no timing; campers step onto the tile.
    Change:
      a. Timing: a future spawn costs  eta + 0.5 * max(0, wait - eta)  and only becomes a "go now"
         job when  wait <= eta + 2  (arrive 1-2 rounds early). Before that the dragon keeps eating
         other pearls; idle dragons may still take it early.
      b. Waiting (F2): when the job tile is within 2 cells and the spawn is more than 1 round away,
         the dragon does not step on it: stepping onto the tile is penalised and staying 1-2 cells
         away is rewarded, so it circles beside the tile (a 2x2 loop for length <= 3). It steps on
         in the spawn round.
      c. Big prizes: tiles that spawn within 3 rounds of each other inside a 3-cell cluster get a
         small cost bonus, so a cluster draws several campers (one per tile, as the matching gives).
    Check: nearest head at spawn and "head adjacent at spawn" in the pipeline (targets <= 3 and >= 20%).

A3. Item 24 - feeding pipeline (partial -> full).
    Now: feeding from r350 (open maps) / r450 (congested), earlier only if behind on queen length;
    feeders die within 4 cells of her.
    Change:
      a. Cap feeding: from round 150, whenever the fleet is at its cap (room_for_more() false, so
         extra pearls cannot become dragons anyway) and we are not in survival mode, surplus dragons
         (not queen, leaders, guards, screens, hunters, defenders; length >= 3) feed the queen.
         Self-throttling: once one feeds, the fleet is below cap and splitting resumes. This keeps
         "numbers first" on congested maps - it only spends what could not become numbers.
      b. Safe queen only: no enemy reported within 10 cells of her in the last 10 rounds.
      c. Placement: when she is in view, the feeder heads for a free cell next to her head (her
         facing is known from vision) and self-destructs there next turn, only if she keeps at least
         2 free exits (exit guard). Otherwise the current "within 4 cells" rule.
      d. While being fed she does not roam: her pearl targets are limited to 6 cells.
      e. The existing late feeding (r350 open / last 50 rounds), giant share and tiebreak rules stay.
    Check: queen length at r200/r300 in local games; she must not die more (quick.sh queen deaths).

A4. Item 26 - half-split at about length 20 (not built).
    Mid-game (before r350) a non-queen dragon of length >= 20 splits in half when there is room for
    more dragons and the child has room (SPLIT_ROOM). On congested maps this includes leaders; on
    open maps leaders and the giant keep growing (length wins there). Survival splits are unchanged.
    Check: count "half-split" in local replays; no rise in wall/self deaths of long dragons.

A5. Item 3 - more enemy sightings (partial -> as full as the rules allow, F1).
    Now: 1 guard slot screens; aimed reports; 3-round report memory; queen status relayed.
    Change:
      a. 2-3 screens: both guard slots screen while the fleet is under 20 dragons (with 20+ the 10%
         slot already gives 2-3).
      b. Screens face the threat: post direction = centre of enemy reports within 15 cells of the
         queen in the last 10 rounds; fall back to the enemy side (mirror of our start).
      c. Inner guards prefer cells on the queen's row or column (a ping only reaches dragons in
         line), so their reports and relays land on her.
    Check: in local replays, count rounds where the queen received an enemy report (log it in her
    status text for the test).

PHASE B - bunker with parked guards
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
B1. Item 6 - guards park on the exposed cells (partial -> full).
    Bunker now happens in three cases: their queen confirmed dead, tie bunker, and v2.12's
    last-40-rounds bunker (the common one).
    Change:
      a. New sonar message: the queen announces her bunker box (cell + round) every turn while in it.
      b. Each guard (and hunters, once their queen is dead) picks a 2x2 "post" next to the box that
         covers the most exposed neighbour cells, skipping posts a teammate already holds, and
         circles it at length <= 3 (a longer guard splits down to 3 first; the child goes free).
         A dragon must move every turn, so a circling length-3 guard keeps 3 of its 4 cells filled.
      c. No dragon of ours ever steps into the queen's box cells.
      d. Guards exist only with 4+ dragons (v2.12 rule), so a tiny fleet is unaffected.
    Check: a forced late bunker (test switch) - posts filled, queen survives.

PHASE C - offence (all gated: never while outnumbered or in survival mode)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
C1. Item 29 - long hunters (not built).
    HUNTER_LEN 4 -> 6 and HUNTER_SPLIT_LEN 8 -> 12 (a hunter at 12 splits 6+6). Below 6 a hunter
    keeps farming. Reach at length 6: 2 free + 4 paid = 6 cells. During expansion hunters still
    split at 4 (FAST_OPEN), so they grow to 6 after it.
C2. Item 30 - lurk outside her vision, from two sides (partial -> full).
    With her position fresh (reported within 4 rounds) but no strike path: each pack member takes a
    side by its rank in the pack (opposite sides first, then the third side) and waits at
    Chebyshev distance 4 from her - just outside her 7x7 view. The moment she steps toward one
    lurker she is in its view and inside its 6-cell reach, and the existing regicide sprint fires.
    No fresh report for 10 rounds: back to searching (current behaviour).
C3. Item 32 - kill their giant (partial -> full).
    Once OUR queen is dead (then only the longest dragon can still win it) and from round 350, if
    their queen has not been seen for 100 rounds (their queens die early, often unseen): prey list
    = their longest dragons first (length >= 10, longest gets the biggest cost bonus). A short
    dragon trading for a giant is a trade up, so SAFE_TRADES allows it.
C4. Item 33 - box their queen in (not built).
    A hunter/lurker that sees their queen but cannot strike prefers moves that take her free exits
    (cells next to her head), as long as our head stays outside her strike reach + 1 and away from
    other enemy heads. Both opponents' queens died when boxed (invalid split, self-hit).
