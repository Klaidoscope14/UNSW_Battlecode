1. THE OPPONENTS' PLAYBOOK (reverse engineered; both bots play the same core game)
=====================================================================================================
  1.1 Churn economy. Split 4 -> 2+2 almost always (BF 132/132 on Default, JM 404/460 on Australia);
      fleets are 50-90% length 2 until the late game. Dragons are disposable: median lifespan
      9-55 rounds, deaths ~= births.
  1.2 Deliberate recycling. A dragon that has eaten what it can (often inside a pocket or a pearl box)
      kills itself next to a teammate, who eats the drop.
        Byte_Force:      by running into its own neck ("hit itself"; e.g. 161 of 184 on WeakHold
                         had a free cell - chosen, not forced).
        Jingling_munmin: by sending the invalid action "split off 1 cell" (all 537 of its
                         "gave no valid action" deaths were split-1 requests).
      Own drops re-eaten within 3 rounds: BF 51 / 87 / 262 (Default / WeakHold / Australia),
      JM 92 / 481 / 751 (Devil / Australia / Slithery).
  1.3 Pocket and box mining. On WeakHold, Prisoners Dilemma, Stripes and Queen of Spades they enter
      dead ends, portal boxes and 1-wide cells, eat, split, and the stub recycles. We refuse those
      cells (sections 2.4, 2.6).
  1.4 Fast, pearl-first opening. Pearls per dragon-turn in rounds 1-50:
            map              us     them
            Stripes          0.0%   17.6%   (BF)
            Prisoners D.     5.2%   18.0%   (JM)
            Slithery        10.0%   21.9%   (JM)
            WeakHold         5.3%   13.8%   (BF)
            Devil            4.2%   13.8%   (JM)
            Trophy           5.3%   13.5%   (JM)
            Australia-JM     1.8%   12.7%
            Queen of Spades  1.7%    5.0%   (BF)
            Australia-BF     1.4%    4.9%
            Default          2.3%    4.1%   (BF)
      After round 50 the per-dragon rates are often close (Devil 12.4 vs 13.7, Australia-BF 5.5 vs
      6.4); the gap is the opening, which then compounds into fleet size (they hold 64 for hundreds
      of rounds; we peak at 5-42 except on Slithery).
      Jingling_munmin also raids: on Australia 35 of its first 52 pearls came from OUR half.
  1.5 Spawn proximity. Median distance from their nearest head to a new pearl: 2-4 cells
      (ours 8-16 in the losses). A dragon of theirs is next to 12-37% of all new pearls.
  1.6 Sprint strikes with length 3. One free plus one paid step gives a length-3 dragon 2 cells of
      reach. Head-ons that came right after a 2-step move: BF 24 at length 3 on Australia; in our
      trades they were the sprinter 36 times on Australia-BF. Multi-step moves: 12-12.5% of their
      turns on Australia (ours 0.9-1.9%). Jingling_munmin also pays length to reach pearls (Slithery:
      457 paid steps, 142 landing on a pearl).
  1.7 Endgame consolidation. Last ~100 rounds: 64 dragons -> 13-23, length 10+ dragons appear
      (Australia-BF longest 15 -> 42 -> 74; Australia-JM 48% of the final fleet is 10+). Byte_Force won
      Australia on exactly this (longest dragon 74 vs our 35).
  1.8 Weak queens. Their queens stay at length 2-4, are never fed, and die by their own hand:
        Devil r60, Trophy r72  - she sent "split off 1 cell" (their recycle action) when boxed in
        Australia-JM r40       - stepped through a portal into her own dragon coming the other way
        Slithery r38           - boxed in by her own swarm, hit a body
        Australia-BF r271      - boxed in by her own swarm at (36,2), hit herself
      Our regicides killed the other three (Default r73, Queen of Spades r77, Prisoners D. r34).
      Jingling_munmin's queen was dead by round 72 in all 5 games; Byte_Force's in 3 of 5.
  1.9 Differences. Jingling_munmin's openings are stronger and it uses paid steps a lot; its queen
      is the weaker of the two. Byte_Force builds a giant at the end; it did badly on Queen of Spades
      (56 pearls all game, fleet max 8), so corridor/portal maps hurt its plain swarm.
  Neither bot prints status text, so all of the above is inferred from moves.

2. WHERE WE LACKED (ranked by how many games it decided)
=====================================================================================================
  2.1 Our own attacks while outnumbered (decided Default, Devil, Prisoners D., Trophy; hurt everywhere).
      122 of our dragons died in SNIPES (v2.7 feature, never benchmarked). 151 of our 164 attack
      deaths (snipe / hunt / attack / regicide / intercept) happened while they already had more
      dragons. A 1-for-1 trade only pays if we out-produce them; we never did. Examples:
        Prisoners D. r7-r26: 5 snipes take the fleet 6/6 -> 3/7; the queen ends up alone.
        Devil: 12 snipes, all while outnumbered; after r86 every death of ours was a snipe or a
               pearl head-on.
        Default: snipes at r46-r68 take us 7 -> 4; the fleet never recovers.
      Hunters also wasted the economy: "hunt" turns 642 (Australia-BF), 735 (Slithery), 322
      (Australia-JM) - mostly after their queen was dead.
  2.2 No survival mode once a game is "won" or the fleet is tiny.
      - We never knew their queen was dead: our queen showed "bunker" 0 times in 10 games.
        In 3 regicides the striker was the only witness and died in the trade; the other 5 deaths
        happened out of sight. The strict death detection (v2.6) cannot fire in these cases.
      - With 1-3 dragons left, they kept hunting, sniping and pearl-chasing in war mode.
  2.3 The queen's deaths (7 of 10 games):
        Trophy r53        L3, "war pearl" for her last 8 rounds, 2-4 enemy heads (and their
                          queen); a sprinting L3-5 hit her.
        Devil r86         L2, inside a 1-wide corridor; one of OUR dragons came down the same
                          corridor from the far end -> boxed in, hit a wall. The exit guard only
                          checks teammates within 2 cells of her.
        Stripes r88       L2, starved (0 pearls), boxed in by their swarm.
        Prisoners D. r137 L3, starved alone (0 pearls in 87 turns), hit by a sprinter.
        Default r298      L2, alone for 100+ rounds among 64 enemies, rammed.
        WeakHold r396     L3, alone, starved, hit by a sprinter.
        Australia-BF r490 L24, fed big, "farm pearl", moved 3 cells in one turn toward a pearl;
                          an L6 enemy 5 cells away (outside her 7x7 view) struck. Their queen had
                          been dead since r271 - 10 more rounds would have won.
  2.4 Queen-safety rules starve a small fleet (decided Stripes, Prisoners D.; hurt WeakHold).
      - Portal avoidance (v2.7) is absolute: on Stripes every pearl box is entered through a portal;
        we ate 0 pearls in 88 rounds.
      - The loop rule (a queen of length <= 3 must always reach a free 2x2) keeps her out of 1-wide
        cells; on Prisoners D. the lone queen ate 0 pearls in 87 turns.
      - At length 2-3 the queen cannot split, so a starving queen can never rebuild a fleet.
      - With a fleet of 2 the other dragon was a GUARD (Stripes): nobody collected at all.
  2.5 Opening (all 10 games, section 1.4). "explore" was 51-63% of our turns in rounds 1-50 on the big
      maps; in races where our head was nearer when the pearl appeared they still won 146
      (Australia-BF), 46 (Australia-JM), 27 of 41 (Default), 14 of 14 (Stripes). On Australia-JM their
      dragons came 37-44 cells for pearls that our explorers 11-17 cells away ignored.
  2.6 Own goals in corridors and pockets.
      - Queen of Spades: 60 non-combat deaths, median age 12 rounds, 28 within 10 rounds of birth,
        55 on corridor/dead-end cells. Pattern: "pearl" -> walks into a pocket -> "save-split" -> the
        2-cell head stub dies.
      - Slithery: 561 non-combat deaths (median age 8, 314 within 10 rounds of birth); 141 times our
        head hit OUR OWN body.
      - WeakHold: all 7 wall deaths on corridor/dead-end cells, some 9-12 rounds after birth.
      - One-step pearls eaten on pocket maps: 28% (WeakHold) and 44% (Queen of Spades) vs their 55%;
        the misses had no enemy near - our survival check refuses the pocket.
  2.7 The endgame giant (Australia-BF): our longest 35 vs 74. Feeding was split between the queen and
      the giant, and the queen's 24 segments were lost with her.

5. WHAT TO INTEGRATE / CHANGE / REMOVE (proposals, for your decision; nothing implemented)
=====================================================================================================
  REMOVE / RESTRICT
  R1. Snipes: switch off, or allow only when we have clearly more dragons than they do and the target
      is long. (122 deaths, 151/164 attacks while outnumbered.)
  R2. Hunting: stop when the enemy queen has not been seen for a while or right after one of our
      regicide strikes; never hunt while outnumbered; never hunt with the last few dragons.
  R3. Guards when the fleet is 1-3 dragons: everyone collects instead.

  CHANGE
  C1. Survival mode. Trigger: fleet <= 3, or fleet < 1/4 of theirs, or "their queen is probably dead"
      (one of ours executed a strike on her head - announce it the turn before striking, since the
      striker's own sonar dies with it), or the last ~40 rounds. In survival mode: the queen hides in a
      safe area away from enemy heads and portals' far ends, no trades of any kind, no multi-step
      charges, no pearl chasing near enemies. Five of our seven losses were games already won.
  C2. Late-game queen (last ~30-50 rounds): no pearl chasing at all, no 2-3 step moves into unseen
      space, stay inside a small known-safe area. Australia-BF was lost at r490 on a pearl charge.
  C3. Starving queen. If the queen is the only eater (or the fleet is tiny) and has not eaten for
      ~20 rounds, relax the portal and loop rules for targets that look safe (no enemy in view,
      short dead end). Stripes and Prisoners D. were lost to zero income.
  C4. Corridor rule for teammates: never enter a 1-wide corridor the queen is in (or heading into),
      from either end. (Devil queen death.)
  C5. Opening: pearl first, explore last. Send each starting dragon to the nearest known pearl or
      spawn cluster; explore only if nothing is known. Do not hunt or snipe in the opening.
  C6. Splits near pockets: no growth split when the child would be born inside a dead end (Queen of
      Spades / WeakHold newborn deaths).

  INTEGRATE (their methods)
  I1. Pocket mining with recycling: allow entering a dead end with pearls when a teammate waits at
      the mouth to eat the drop (or accept the stub's death as recycling). This is how both bots
      out-ate us on WeakHold, Stripes and Prisoners D.
  I2. Free recycling: let a short dragon with nothing reachable to eat self-destruct next to a
      teammate (theirs do it hundreds of times per game; ours only when boxed in).
  I3. Paid steps to win pearl races (Jingling_munmin: 142 pearls on paid steps on Slithery).
  I4. Endgame giant via consolidation - already in v2.10/2.11; feed the giant harder once the queen
      is in survival mode (Australia-BF lost on longest dragon).

  EXPLOIT
  E1. Their queens kill themselves when crowded (5 of 10 games). We do not need to hunt them: survive,
      keep our fleet, and let their swarm box their queen in.
  E2. Their sprinters have 2 cells of reach at length 3: keep our queen and small fleet 3+ cells from
      any unseen-but-reported head; do not walk into lines of their L3s.
