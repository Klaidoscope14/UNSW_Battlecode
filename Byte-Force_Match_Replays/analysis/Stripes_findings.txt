STRIPES vs Byte_Force  (v2.11.0)  -  LOSS by elimination at round 88
Map 24x12. Horizontal kelp stripes; most pearls sit in 2x2 kelp boxes whose only entrances are
PORTAL edges. 16 portal edges. We started (team A) with 2 dragons of length 2.
Raw numbers: analysis/raw/Stripes_{metrics,deep,econ,profile}.txt

HEADLINE
  We ate ZERO pearls in 88 rounds. We never split (needs length 4), so the fleet stayed at 2 while
  Byte_Force went 2 -> 17 and ate 76 pearls (62 of them on our half).

WHY WE ATE NOTHING
  1. The queen avoids portals (v2.7 rule: -1,000,000 for crossing a portal, -150,000 per portal edge
     next to her destination) and unexplored areas. The pearl boxes can only be entered through
     portals, so she never went in. Her status was "pearl" 86 of 88 rounds — she had targets, but
     every path to them crossed a portal.
  2. Our only other dragon (#2) was a GUARD: it stayed near the queen ("guard", "intercept") and never
     went into a box either. It died at round 39 in an intercept head-on.
  3. Kelp-aware check: our heads never had a reachable (one-step) pearl in the whole game. The 36 times
     a pearl looked adjacent it was across kelp.
  4. In 14 pearl races our head was nearer when it appeared; they won all 14 (via portals).

WHAT BYTE_FORCE DID
  - 38 portal jumps; entered the boxes, ate, split 4 -> 2+2 (25) or 5 -> 2+3 (8), and the stubs died
    inside by hitting themselves (19 "hit itself", 6 by round 49) — boxes mined and recycled.
  - Their head was 3 cells from a new pearl (median) vs 8 for us; adjacent to 12% of spawns.
  - Their swarm then hunted us: by r80-87 four of their heads were within 1-4 cells of our queen; she
     died "doomed" (boxed in, hit a body) at round 88.

WHERE WE LACKED
  A. A queen-safety rule (avoid portals) that is absolute even when the team has no other eater.
  B. A guard role assigned when the fleet is only 2 dragons — nobody was collecting.
  C. No "economic emergency": length 2, no pearls, fleet of 2 for 40+ rounds should change behaviour.
