     their head on a corridor/dead-end cell. The pattern before death: "pearl" -> "save-split" or

  C. Pearls next to a head left uneaten: 181 eaten of 415 one-step pearls (44%); 183 of the misses had
     no enemy near — the survival check refuses pocket cells.
  D. Hunting kept going: 66 hunt turns, 6 snipes, 1 attack, after their queen was already dead.
  E. No bunker after the regicide (the striker was the only witness and died).

WHAT WORKED
