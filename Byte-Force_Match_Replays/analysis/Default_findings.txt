HEADLINE
  We killed their queen at round 73 (regicide by our #18, length 4, which died in the trade). From
  then on our queen alive at round 500 was a win. Instead we were eliminated: our fleet went 8 -> 3 by
  round 75 and to just the queen by round 190; 64 enemy length-2 dragons finally rammed her at 298.

WHAT BYTE_FORCE DOES
  1. Pure swarm: 132 splits, every one 4 -> 2+2. 71-91% of their fleet is length 2 all game.
  2. Production continued at full speed without a queen (53 splits in r100-199, 54 in r200-298).
     Losing the queen did not slow them at all.
  3. Their head was 2 cells from a new pearl (median) vs 13 for us; a dragon of theirs was next to
     31% of all new pearls.
  4. Recycling: 23 "hit itself" deaths, 18 of them with a free cell, a teammate within 3, drop
     re-eaten within 3 rounds. 26 more of theirs died running into their own bodies (crowding).
  5. Their queen was a plain length 2-4 dragon wandering (median 14 cells from her start).

WHERE WE LACKED
  A. Trades we could not afford. 13 of our dragons died, all head-on. 7 of them were our own attacks
     (5 snipe, 1 hunt, 1 regicide) and 6 of those 7 were made while they already had more dragons
     (fleet 7/10, 6/11, 5/10, 4/9, 3/20, 2/47). The snipes of length 2-4 at r46-r68 cut us from 7 to 4.
  B. Economy. 20 pearls in 298 rounds vs 349. Our dragons were near pearls (100% within 1 of a spawn
     tile — all tiles spawn here) but 27 pearls were taken by them although we were nearer; in those
     races our nearest dragon was busy with snipe 5, hunt-queen 2, regicide 2 or just lost the race.
     Pearls per dragon-turn: 2.3% (us) vs 4.1% (them) in rounds 1-50.
  C. The death of their queen was invisible: the only dragon of ours that saw her (the striker #18)
     died in the same head-on, so no bunker, no change of plan.
  D. The lone queen. From round 190 our queen (length 2) was alone. She stayed on "pearl" (100% of
     her status texts after r200) but ate almost nothing; enemy heads were within 3 of her in 77
     rounds; she never stopped to hide. Being length 2 she could not split to rebuild.

WHAT WOULD HAVE CHANGED THE RESULT
  - No snipes/hunts while outnumbered -> keep the fleet (we had 8 at r25, equal to theirs).
  - Treat a regicide strike as "their queen is probably dead": we only had to survive 225 more rounds.
  - A survival mode for a tiny fleet: queen + last dragons avoid every enemy head, never trade.
