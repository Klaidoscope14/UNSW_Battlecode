WHAT BYTE_FORCE DOES (evidence)
  1. Split 4 -> 2+2, always (217 of 292 splits had a child of 2; 196 at parent length 4).
     Fleet is mostly length 2-3 until round 300 (r200: 58% L2, 25% L3).
  2. Recycling by self-collision: 123 "hit itself" deaths, most with a free cell available and a
     teammate within 3; 262 of their own drops re-eaten within 3 rounds.
  3. Sprint strikes with length-3 dragons: 1 free + 1 paid step = 2 cells of reach. 24 of their
     head-ons came straight after a 2-step move at length 3; in 36 of our trades they sprinted and we
     were walking. Multi-step moves: 12% of their turns vs 1.9% of ours.
  4. Endgame consolidation into a giant: from round 400 the fleet shrinks 57 -> 13 while the longest
     grows 15 -> 42 -> 74 (r499: 31% L6-9, 23% L10+). That giant won the tiebreak.
  5. Their queen stays short (max 4) and is never fed; she sits among her own dragons (that is what
     killed her: boxed in at (36,2) under a row of her own bodies).
  6. Portals: 102 jumps vs our 3.

WHERE WE LACKED
  A. Opening. Rounds 1-50: 7 pearls for us vs 35 (1.4% vs 4.9% pearls per dragon-turn). 63% of our
     opening turns were "explore". 146 pearls were taken by them although our head was nearer when the
     pearl appeared (our nearest dragon was on "pearl" 77, "hunt" 32, "explore" 13, "snipe" 11).
  B. Attacks while outnumbered. 48 of our dragons died in attacks (snipe 32, hunt 12, attack 4), every
     one of them while they already had more dragons. 642 turns were spent on "hunt" — hunters looking
     for a queen that was long dead after r271.
  C. The queen at the end. Feeding (open-map consolidation) grew her 9 -> 24 between r400 and r490 and
     she roamed (median 30 cells from her start; she stayed in a 4-cell area only 5% of the time).
     At r489, length 24, she moved 3 cells in one turn towards a pearl ("farm pearl"); an L6 enemy 5
     cells away (outside her 7x7 view) struck the next round. No report of it reached her.
  D. We never knew their queen was dead (no dragon of ours saw her die), so no bunker. But even
     without knowing: in the last ~30 rounds a long queen has nothing to gain from pearls and
     everything to lose — this is where the game was lost.
  E. Our own giant: longest 35 at r500 vs their 74. Our feeding split between queen and giant; the
     queen's 24 segments vanished with her.

V2.11 FEATURES IN THIS GAME

WHAT WOULD HAVE CHANGED THE RESULT
  - Queen in pure-safety mode for the last ~30-50 rounds (no pearl chasing, no multi-step charges,
    stay within a small safe area) -> win on queen length (theirs was dead).
  - Fewer pointless hunts/snipes -> more eaters -> bigger fleet -> bigger giant.
